Once you have activated Page Shield, you can set up one or more alerts informing you of relevant client-side changes on your zones.

You can configure unscoped or scoped alerts:

- **Unscoped alert**: An alert configured for all zones in your Cloudflare account. Scoped alerts are trigged either daily, hourly, or immediately, depending on the [alert type](/page-shield/reference/alerts/).

- **Scoped alert**: An alert scoped to one or more zones. You must configure [policies](/page-shield/policies/) for the zones you select to receive any notifications. Scoped alerts are triggered immediately. Policy violations will not trigger an alert.

  :::note
  If you set up a scoped alert for a zone and you do not configure any [allow policy](/page-shield/policies/#policy-actions) for that zone, you will not receive any alerts.
  :::

For alerts sent at regular intervals, you might experience a delay between adding a new script and receiving an alert.
