To revoke a member's access to your account, or to cancel a pending invitation:

1. In the Cloudflare dashboard, go to the **Members** page.

    <DashButton url="/?to=/:account/members" />

3. On the member's row, select **...** > **Remove member**.

4. In the confirmation dialog, select **Remove member**.

</TabItem> <TabItem label="API">

To revoke a member's access to your account using the API, send a [`DELETE` request](/api/resources/accounts/subresources/members/methods/delete/).
