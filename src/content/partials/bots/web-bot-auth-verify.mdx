Send a signed request to `https://crawltest.com/cdn-cgi/web-bot-auth`. Add `https://crawltest.com` to your allowed origins first.

| Status | Meaning | Next step |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `200` | Cloudflare recognizes the key and verifies the signature. | Test your destination URL. |
| `400` | Cloudflare cannot process the signed request. | Check the three signature headers and covered components. |
| `401` | The key is unknown, or signature verification failed. | Check registration, `keyid`, clock, and the exact outgoing URL. |

A `401` does not prove your signature is correct. A local signature test checks the code; a `200` checks your registered identity.

Web Bot Auth proves identity. Website owners still control access to their sites.
