## Requirements

All domains using Cloudflare Registrar must use Cloudflare for authoritative DNS on a [full setup](/dns/zone-setups/full-setup/)[^1]. This means [updating your nameservers](https://developers.cloudflare.com/dns/nameservers/update-nameservers/) to point to Cloudflare. You cannot change to another DNS provider while using Cloudflare Registrar.

[^1]: Meaning Cloudflare is your primary, authoritative DNS provider.
