<Details header="Allowlist Cloudflare IP addresses">

Explicitly block all traffic that does not come from [Cloudflare IP addresses](/fundamentals/concepts/cloudflare-ip-addresses/) (or the IP addresses of your trusted partners, vendors, or applications).

Cloudflare IP ranges are shared across customers. Combine this filter with [origin authentication](/fundamentals/reference/http-headers/#trust-visitor-ip-headers) to identify traffic for your account or zone.

* **Security**: Moderately secure.
* **Availability**: All customers.
* **Challenges**:
  * Requires allowlisting Cloudflare IP ranges at your origin server.
  * Allows connections from other Cloudflare customers. A Cloudflare source IP does not prove that your zone's security rules ran.


</Details>
