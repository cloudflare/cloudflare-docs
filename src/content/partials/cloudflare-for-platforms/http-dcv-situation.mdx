Specific (non-wildcard) custom hostnames can use [HTTP-based DCV](/cloudflare-for-platforms/cloudflare-for-saas/security/certificate-management/issue-and-validate/validate-certificates/http/) for certificate renewals when:

* The hostname is pointing to the SaaS provider.
* The hostname's traffic is proxying through the Cloudflare network.
* The hostname does not use [Delegated DCV](/cloudflare-for-platforms/cloudflare-for-saas/security/certificate-management/issue-and-validate/validate-certificates/delegated-dcv/).

Cloudflare may use HTTP validation even if you selected TXT for initial issuance. If your custom hostnames do not meet these requirements, retrieve the current `ssl.method` and `ssl.validation_records`. These fields identify whether to use manual HTTP or TXT validation.
