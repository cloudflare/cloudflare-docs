Before you begin, [set up Cloudflare Mesh](/mesh/get-started/) for your account. The setup wizard configures device enrollment, Mesh IP assignment, enrolled-device reachability, and Gateway proxying.

If your account already uses Cloudflare One, verify the [required account settings](/mesh/get-started/#required-account-settings). Existing settings are not overwritten by the setup wizard.
