Dashboard log streams are only available for <GlossaryTooltip term="remotely-managed tunnel">remotely-managed tunnels</GlossaryTooltip>. To view logs from the dashboard:

1. In [Cloudflare One](https://one.dash.cloudflare.com/), go to **Networks** > **Connectors** > **Cloudflare Tunnels**.
2. Select **View logs** next to the tunnel you want to monitor.
3. (Optional) To stream logs from a specific replica, select its **Connector ID** in the **Connectors** list on the tunnel overview page instead.
