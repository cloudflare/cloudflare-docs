A `1033` error indicates your tunnel is not connected to Cloudflare's network because Cloudflare's network cannot find a healthy `cloudflared` instance to receive the traffic.

First, review whether your tunnel is listed as `Active` in the [Cloudflare dashboard](https://dash.cloudflare.com/) by going to **Networking** > **Tunnels** or run `cloudflared tunnel list`. If the tunnel is not `Active`, review the following and take the action necessary for your tunnel status:

<Render file="tunnel/tunnel-status" product="cloudflare-one" />

If your tunnel is `Active` but you still receive `1033` errors, verify that your DNS record points to the correct tunnel subdomain. On accounts in Cloudflare's [FedRAMP High](https://www.cloudflare.com/cloudflare-for-government/) environment, CNAME records must point to `<UUID>.fed-cfargotunnel.com`. A CNAME record that points to `<UUID>.cfargotunnel.com` returns `1033` errors even when the tunnel is healthy. For more information, refer to [DNS records](/tunnel/concepts/routing/#dns-records).
