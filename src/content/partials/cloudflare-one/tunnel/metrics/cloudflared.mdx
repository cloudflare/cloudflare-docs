:::note[Transport visibility by protocol]
`cloudflared` exports per-connection (`conn_index`) packet loss (`quic_client_lost_packets`), frame counts including `Ack` frames (`quic_client_sent_frames` and `quic_client_received_frames`), RTT, and congestion metrics for `quic` connections.

When `cloudflared` connects over `http2`, it does not expose TCP-level packet loss, retransmission, or ACK metrics — use host OS networking tools (such as `ss` or packet captures) to inspect the underlying TCP connection. Cloudflare does not expose edge-side tunnel transport metrics.
:::
