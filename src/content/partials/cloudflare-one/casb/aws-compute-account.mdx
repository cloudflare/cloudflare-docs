To connect a compute account to your AWS integration:

1. In [Cloudflare dashboard](https://dash.cloudflare.com/), go to **Zero Trust** > **Cloud & SaaS findings** > **Integrations**.

   <DashButton url="/?to=/:account/integrations/cloud-and-saas" zeroTrust />

2. Find and select your AWS integration.
3. Select **Open connection instructions**.
4. Follow the instructions provided to connect a new compute account.
5. Select **Refresh**.
