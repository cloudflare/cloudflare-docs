:::note

As of compatibility date `2026-08-04`, the `nodejs_compat` flag is enabled by default. If your compatibility date is earlier, add the `nodejs_compat` compatibility flag to your [Wrangler configuration file](/workers/wrangler/configuration/) to opt in. This also enables `nodejs_compat_v2` as long as your compatibility date is `2024-09-23` or later. Refer to the [Node.js compatibility flag](/workers/configuration/compatibility-flags/#nodejs-compatibility-flag) for details.

:::
