---
title: 1 GB storage limit for new Artifacts repos
description: Artifacts will enforce a 1 GB storage limit for repos created on or after September 29, 2026.
products:
  - artifacts
date: 2026-09-29
---

Starting September 29, 2026, [Artifacts](/artifacts/) will enforce a **1 GB storage limit per repo** for repos created on or after that date.

Repos created before September 29, 2026, will retain their 10 GB storage limit.

For more information, refer to [Artifacts limits](/artifacts/platform/limits/).
