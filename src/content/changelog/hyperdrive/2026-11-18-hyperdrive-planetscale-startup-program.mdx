Customers in the [Cloudflare Startup Program](https://www.cloudflare.com/startups/) can now create PlanetScale Postgres and MySQL databases from Cloudflare, with usage billed through their Cloudflare account.

Startup credits applied to PlanetScale usage is limited for each tier. PlanetScale usage beyond a tier's limit is billed to your account.

| Tier   | PlanetScale credit limit |
| ------ | ---------------------- |
| Tier 3 | $2,500                 |
| Tier 2 | $10,000                |
| Tier 1 | $50,000                |

Check your program tier to confirm the limit that applies to your account.

To start using it, open the Hyperdrive section of the dashboard. The **Create PlanetScale** button appears there for eligible accounts.

<DashButton url="/?to=/:account/workers/hyperdrive?modal=1&type=planetscale&step=1" />

For database setup and how Hyperdrive connects Workers to PlanetScale, refer to [PlanetScale Postgres and MySQL with Hyperdrive](/hyperdrive/planetscale/).
