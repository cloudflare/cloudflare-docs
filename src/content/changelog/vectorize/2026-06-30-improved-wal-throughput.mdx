---

We have greatly improved the throughput of the Vectorize [write-ahead log (WAL)](https://blog.cloudflare.com/building-vectorize-a-distributed-vector-database-on-cloudflare-developer-platform/#the-wal). As a result, the end-to-end latency for a vector change to become queryable has been significantly reduced: median latency has dropped from 2 minutes to under 30 seconds, and p99 latency from 5 minutes to under 2 minutes.

![Vectorize p99 WAL batch end-to-end latency improved](~/assets/images/vectorize/vectorize-p99-wal-batch-end-to-end-latency-improvement.png)

This means inserts, upserts, and deletes are reflected in query results faster, improving the freshness of semantic search, recommendation, and retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) workloads. No changes to your code or configuration are required to benefit from this improvement.

Refer to the [Vectorize documentation](/vectorize/) to learn more.
