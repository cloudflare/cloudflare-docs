# Adjustment to Final Disposition Column

The **Final Disposition** column of the **Team Submissions > Reclassifications** page inside the **Email Security Zero Trust** application is changing for non-Phishguard customers.
---

### What's Changing

- Column will be called **Status** instead of **Final Disposition**
- Column status values will now be: **Submitted** or **Accepted** or **Rejected**

### Next Steps

We will listen carefully to your feedback and continue to refine comprehensive ways to communicate updates on your submissions. Your submissions are continuing to be addressed at an even greater rate than before, fueling faster and more accurate email security improvement.
