---
title: LLM Summaries for Detections
description: Cloudy summaries for detections are now available in the Cloudflare dashboard
date: 2025-12-01T21:11:33Z
---

When we brought Cloudflare Email Security into the Cloudflare dashboard, we received feedback that our detections could feel vague. Customers often asked what specifically caused a message to be labeled as malicious, spam, or suspicious.

We took that feedback seriously.

Now, within the **Investigation** page for an email, you will see **Cloudy summaries** that explain the detections that triggered on the message. These summaries appear in the **Reasons for disposition** section, inside a new orange box labeled **Reasons summary by Cloudy**. The summary provides a plain-language explanation of why the email was flagged.

For customers who want the full technical detail, nothing has been removed. You can still click **View all signatures** to see every model, rule, and signal that triggered on the message, just as before.

**These summaries are not trained on customer data.** They are generated using the outputs of our existing detection models and analysis systems, translating those signals into clearer explanations.

This feature is available for all Email Security packages:
- **Advantage**
- **Enterprise**
- **Enterprise + PhishGuard**
