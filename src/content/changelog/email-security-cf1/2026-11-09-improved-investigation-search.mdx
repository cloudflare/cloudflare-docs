---
title: Improved investigation search
description: Use keywords, typed filters, and quick filters to find email messages faster.
date: 2026-11-09
---

Email Security now provides a unified search bar for investigations. It replaces the previous Email Screener side modal and makes searches easier to refine.

Search with free-text keywords or `field:value` filters. Autocomplete suggests filter names and values as you type. Add terms, press Enter, and rerun searches as you refine results. Select **From**, **To**, **Disposition**, **Status**, or **Threat type** values in the results to narrow your search.

Narrow the results with a date preset or choose your own range. The new search is available to all Email Security customers.
