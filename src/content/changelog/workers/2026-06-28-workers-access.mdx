You can now protect a Worker with Cloudflare Access directly. Enabling Access on a Worker requires sign-in before anyone can reach it, and automatically covers every domain associated with it: routes, Custom Domains, and its `workers.dev` hostname. Previously, you had to create separate Access applications for each hostname or path. Now you configure it once on the Worker itself through the dashboard.

**Choose what to protect**

- **Protect all Workers**: Require sign-in for every Worker in your account. You can choose to protect all production and preview deployments, or only preview deployments.
<Image src={protectAllWorkers} alt="Account-wide Access setting that protects all Workers" />

- **Protect a single Worker**: Require sign-in for one specific Worker across all of its domains and previews, or only its previews.
<Image src={protectOneWorker} alt="Access setting for protecting a single Worker" />

- **Make a Worker public**: If Access is enabled for all Workers on your account, you can make a specific Worker public by adding a Worker-level bypass.
<Image src={makeWorkerPublic} alt="Bypass setting to make a Worker public" />

**Control who can sign in**

Define who can access your Workers by Cloudflare account membership, email address, or email domain.
<Image src={chooseWhoCanSignIn} alt="Access policy configuration for controlling who can sign in" />

You can also [configure Access through the API](/workers/configuration/cloudflare-access/) and validate the Access JWT inside your Worker for identity-aware logic.

For setup instructions, refer to [Cloudflare Access for Workers](/workers/configuration/cloudflare-access/).
