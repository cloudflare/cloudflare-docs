Cloudflare now keeps [versions](/workers/versions-and-deployments/) of your Workers and [deployments](/pages/configuration/preview-deployments/) of your Pages projects for 30 days. After 30 days, they are deleted, except your 100 most recent and what is currently deployed.

**What this does not affect:**

- **Your running applications:** Deployed Worker versions and your current Pages production deployment are always kept, regardless of age.
- **Rollbacks:** You can still [roll back](/workers/versions-and-deployments/rollbacks/) to any of your 100 most recent versions.
- **Worker Previews:** A [Worker Preview](/workers/previews/) URL always points to the latest deployment of that Preview, so it keeps working as long as the Preview exists.

**What you will notice:**

- **A shorter history:** Versions and deployments older than 30 days no longer appear in the dashboard or API, unless they are one of your 100 most recent or are currently deployed.
- **Workers Version URLs:** A version's [Version URL](/workers/versions-and-deployments/version-urls/), including any `--preview-alias`, is removed with the version.
- **Pages preview URLs:** A deployment's [preview URL](/pages/configuration/preview-deployments/) is removed with the deployment.

### Use Previews, not Version URLs, for branches and pull requests

Version URLs are not Previews. A Version URL runs one uploaded version against your production resources and is removed with its version. For branches and pull requests, use [Previews](/workers/previews/), which have their own URL and resources and update every time you push.

For more information, refer to [Compare workflows](/workers/previews/compare-workflows/), [Workers version retention](/workers/versions-and-deployments/#version-retention), and [Pages deployment limits](/pages/platform/limits/#deployments).
