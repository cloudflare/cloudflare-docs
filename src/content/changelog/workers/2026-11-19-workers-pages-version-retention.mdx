Cloudflare now keeps [versions](/workers/versions-and-deployments/) of your Workers and [deployments](/pages/configuration/preview-deployments/) of your Pages projects for 30 days. After 30 days, they are deleted, except your 100 most recent and what is currently deployed.

**What is not affected:**

- **Running applications:** Deployed Worker versions and your current Pages production deployment are always kept.
- **Rollbacks:** You can still [roll back](/workers/versions-and-deployments/rollbacks/) to any of your 100 most recent versions.
- **Worker Previews:** A [Worker Preview](/workers/previews/) URL always points to its latest deployment, so it keeps working.

**What changes:**

- Older versions and deployments no longer appear in the dashboard or API.
- Their URLs stop working, including Worker [Version URLs](/workers/versions-and-deployments/version-urls/) (and any `--preview-alias`) and Pages [preview URLs](/pages/configuration/preview-deployments/).

For branches and pull requests, use [Worker Previews](/workers/previews/). Unlike Version URLs, which run against your production resources, each Worker Preview has its own resources and a URL that updates with every push.

For more information, refer to [Compare workflows](/workers/previews/compare-workflows/), [Workers version retention](/workers/versions-and-deployments/#version-retention), and [Pages deployment limits](/pages/platform/limits/#deployments).
