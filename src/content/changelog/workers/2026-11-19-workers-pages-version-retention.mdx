Cloudflare now keeps [versions](/workers/versions-and-deployments/) of your Workers and [deployments](/pages/configuration/preview-deployments/) of your Pages projects for 30 days. After 30 days, they are deleted, except your 100 most recent and what is currently deployed.

**What is not affected:**

- **Running applications:** Deployed Worker versions and your current Pages production deployment are always kept
- **Rollbacks:** You can still [roll back](/workers/versions-and-deployments/rollbacks/) to any of your 100 most recent Worker versions, and to any Pages [production deployment](/pages/configuration/rollbacks/) that has not been deleted
- **Worker Previews:** [Worker Preview](/workers/previews/) URLs always serve the latest deployment of that Preview. Use Worker Previews for branches and pull requests instead of Version URLs, which run with your production bindings

**What you will notice:**

- Deleted versions and deployments no longer appear in the dashboard or API
- The URL associated with each deleted Worker version, known as its [Version URL](/workers/versions-and-deployments/version-urls/) (including any `--preview-alias`), stops working, as does the [preview URL](/pages/configuration/preview-deployments/) of each deleted Pages deployment

For more information, refer to [Workers version retention](/workers/versions-and-deployments/#version-retention), [Pages deployment limits](/pages/platform/limits/#deployments), and [Compare workflows](/workers/previews/compare-workflows/).
