---
title: More pending operations keep Durable Objects alive
description: More pending operations prevent Durable Object eviction for up to 15 minutes each.
products:
  - durable-objects
date: 2026-09-15
---

Pending service binding fetches now prevent Durable Object eviction. Requests to another Durable Object through remote procedure call (RPC) or `fetch()` and `this.ctx.container.monitor()` also prevent eviction.

Previously, these operations did not prevent eviction after the standard inactivity period. Pending work could stop after the caller disconnected.

Outbound `fetch()` requests to external services, TCP sockets, and outbound WebSockets already prevent eviction. Each listed operation prevents eviction for up to 15 minutes.

Starting another operation that prevents eviction later can extend time in memory. There is no 15-minute limit for the entire invocation.

![Timeline showing a service binding fetch, Durable Object RPC, and monitor() each preventing eviction for up to 15 minutes. Later operations extend time in memory.](~/assets/images/durable-objects/pending-io-keep-alive.svg)

This helps Durable Objects continue asynchronous work after the caller disconnects. Duration charges continue while an operation prevents eviction.

For more information, refer to [Lifecycle of a Durable Object](/durable-objects/concepts/durable-object-lifecycle/).
