The [Billable Usage dashboard](/changelog/post/2026-04-13-billable-usage-dashboard-and-budget-alerts/) now includes a new **filters sidebar**, allowing you to cut through the noise and focus exactly on the costs that matter most to your team.

## What changed

- **Granular filtering:** Narrow your usage view by broad **product families** (such as Workers or R2) or drill down into **individual products**.
- **Context-aware options:** The product selection dynamically narrows based on the families you select, keeping your workflow intuitive and relevant.
- **Unified dashboard sync:** Applying a filter instantly updates your summary totals, daily usage bar chart, cost breakdown, and detailed usage table simultaneously.

<Aside type="tip">
Need to start over? Select **Reset** to instantly clear all active filters and return to your full, account-wide view.
</Aside>
