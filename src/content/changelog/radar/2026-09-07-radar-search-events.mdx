[**Cloudflare Radar**](/radar/) search now includes Internet events and outages alongside existing results. Search event descriptions or related entities, such as locations, ASes, bots, and top-level domains, to find relevant events and open the most relevant Radar view.

![Radar search results showing Internet outage events associated with locations and autonomous systems](~/assets/images/radar/radar-search-events.png)

Event links preserve the event date range, making it easier to investigate what changed before, during, and after an event. These results are also available to browser-based AI agents through [WebMCP](/browser-run/features/webmcp/).
