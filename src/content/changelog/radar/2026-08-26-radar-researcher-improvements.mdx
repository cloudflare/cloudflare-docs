[**Cloudflare Radar**](/radar/) expands the [Radar Researcher](https://radar.cloudflare.com/?prompt=) beta with richer sources and new ways to investigate Internet data.

## Connected insights

Radar Researcher responses can now link to relevant Radar pages, reports, and Cloudflare Blog posts.

![Radar Researcher response linking to the IP Address Information and Network Quality Test pages](~/assets/images/radar/radar-researcher-page-links.png)

## URL Scanner report explanations

Select **Explain with AI** on a [URL Scanner report](https://radar.cloudflare.com/scan) to have Radar Researcher explain its findings and answer follow-up questions about the scanned site.

![Radar Researcher explaining findings from an example.com URL Scanner report](~/assets/images/radar/radar-researcher-url-scanner-explanation.png)

## Improved shared sessions

Shared conversations now open in fullscreen, while the share URL remains available until you close the panel or start a new conversation.

Open [Radar Researcher](https://radar.cloudflare.com/?prompt=) to explore these improvements.
