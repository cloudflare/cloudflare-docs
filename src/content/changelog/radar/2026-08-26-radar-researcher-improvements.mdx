[**Cloudflare Radar**](/radar/) expands the [Radar Researcher](https://radar.cloudflare.com/?prompt=) beta with richer sources and new ways to investigate Internet data:

- **Connected insights** — responses can now link to relevant Radar pages, reports, and Cloudflare Blog posts.
- **URL Scanner explanations** — select **Explain with AI** from a [URL Scanner report](https://radar.cloudflare.com/scan) to start a conversation grounded in its screenshot and scan details.
- **Improved shared sessions** — shared conversations now open in fullscreen, while the share URL remains available until you close the panel or start a new conversation.

Open [Radar Researcher](https://radar.cloudflare.com/?prompt=) to explore these improvements.
