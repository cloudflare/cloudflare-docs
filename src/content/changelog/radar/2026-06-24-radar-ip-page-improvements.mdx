[**Radar**](/radar/) now plots your IPv4 and IPv6 locations on the [IP page](https://radar.cloudflare.com/ip), shows the Cloudflare data centers serving your connection, and includes more detail about the autonomous system (AS) your IP belongs to.

## Your IP location on the map

The map of your connection now shows:

- **Your IPv4 and IPv6 location markers** — your primary IP in red and your secondary IP in blue. When both addresses geolocate to the same place, they collapse into a single marker, with a note explaining why IPv4 and IPv6 can resolve to different locations.
- **Cloudflare data center markers** — every Cloudflare location as a dot, with the data center you connected to highlighted.
- **A connecting line** from each of your IP markers to the Cloudflare data center serving your connection.

![Cloudflare Radar IP page map showing IPv4 and IPv6 location markers, Cloudflare data center dots, and connecting lines](~/assets/images/radar/ip-page-location-map.png)

Due to the data policies of our geolocation provider, this detailed location is only available for your own IP. Other IP addresses keep the current country-level view.

## Extended AS information

The AS card on the IP page now shows additional detail about the network an IP belongs to — including alternate names, the operator website, and an estimate of the AS user population — alongside the AS number and country.

![Cloudflare Radar IP page AS card showing AS number, alternate names, country, operator website, and estimated user population](~/assets/images/radar/ip-page-as-card.png)

Visit the [Cloudflare Radar IP page](https://radar.cloudflare.com/ip) to explore more details about your IP.
