The [**Cloudflare Radar**](/radar/) homepage has been redesigned to make global Internet data easier to explore for broader audiences, including journalists, policymakers, and everyday users, while preserving the depth that technical users rely on.

![Animation scrolling through the redesigned Cloudflare Radar homepage](~/assets/images/radar/radar-homepage-redesign.gif)

- **Interactive map**: Shows Internet traffic and outages around the world, and acts as a starting point for exploring specific countries and trends.
- **Curated insights section**: Tabbed navigation that highlights summary statistics across Radar data, with a call to action to explore each area in depth.
- **Updated interface**: Built with [Kumo](https://kumo-ui.com/) components, the same ones used across Cloudflare products.

The rest of Radar will be migrated to the new design over time. Share feedback at [radar@cloudflare.com](mailto:radar@cloudflare.com).

For more details, read the [blog post](https://blog.cloudflare.com/radar-redesign/) or visit [Cloudflare Radar](https://radar.cloudflare.com/).
