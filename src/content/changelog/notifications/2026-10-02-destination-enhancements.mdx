---
title: Webhooks on all plans and coding agent destinations
description: Webhook destinations are now available on all plans, including Free. Coding agents are now supported as alert destinations.
date: 2026-10-02
---

Webhook destinations are now available on every Cloudflare plan, including Free. Previously, webhooks required a Pro plan or higher. Accounts without a paid zone can configure up to 100 webhook destinations.

Coding agents are also now supported as alert destinations. You can deliver alerts directly to supported coding agents alongside email, webhooks, and PagerDuty.

To configure destinations, go to **Observability** > **Alerts** > **Destinations** in the Cloudflare dashboard. For setup details, refer to [Configure webhooks](/notifications/get-started/configure-webhooks/).
