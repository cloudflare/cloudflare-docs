The **Cloudflare Tunnel - Granular Connector cloudflared Read And Monitor** role is now generally available. You can grant a member read access and log streaming for specific [Cloudflare Tunnel](/tunnel/) instances without granting write access or access to every Tunnel in your account.

This role includes the same access as **Cloudflare Tunnel - Granular Connector cloudflared Read**: viewing selected Tunnels, their IP and hostname routes, and connector host details. It also grants [remote log streaming](/tunnel/observability/#remote-log-streaming), so support operators can troubleshoot a Tunnel without changing its configuration. The existing **Read** role does not grant log streaming access.

To assign the role, follow [Grant a granular permission](/tunnel/guides/granular-permissions/#grant-a-granular-permission). For the available roles, refer to the [resource-scoped roles reference](/fundamentals/manage-members/roles/#resource-scoped-roles).
