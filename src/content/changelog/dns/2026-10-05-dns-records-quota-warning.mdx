---
title: Warnings when approaching your DNS records quota
description: The DNS records page now warns you before you reach your DNS records quota.
date: 2026-10-05
---

The DNS records page in the Cloudflare dashboard now shows a warning once you have used 85% of your [DNS records quota](/dns/manage-dns-records/#dns-records-quota).

The warning reflects the quota that applies to you. If your zone has its own quota, the warning shows that zone's usage. If your account uses an [account-level DNS records quota](/dns/manage-dns-records/#per-account-quota), the warning shows your usage across all zones in the account.
