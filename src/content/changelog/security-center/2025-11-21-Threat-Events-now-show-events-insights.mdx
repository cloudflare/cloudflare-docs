title: Threat insights are now avaoilable in the Threat Events platfrom
description: Threat events users can now access threat insights curaated by our threat intelligence analysts.
date: 2025-11-21
---

The threat events platform now has threat insights available for some relevant parent events and threat intelligence analyst users can access these insights for their threat hunting activity. 
Insights are also flagged in the dashboard by a small `lightning icon` and the insights can refer to multiple, connected events, potentially part of the same attack or campaign and associated with the same threat actor.  

For more information, check out our [threat events platform documentation](security-center/cloudforce-one/#analyze-threat-events).
