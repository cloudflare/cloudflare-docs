We are refreshing the Workers AI model catalog to make room for new releases and updated models. Update your apps to remove references to the models listed below before November 20, 2026. We originally stated these models would be deprecated on November 15, 2026, and have moved the deprecation date to November 20, 2026.

Starting November 20, 2026, these models will stop returning responses. Requests to them will fail with an HTTP `410 Gone` error and [error code `5028`](/workers-ai/platform/errors/).

We recommend moving to newer models for the best token efficiency and throughput. For pricing, refer to the [Workers AI pricing page](/workers-ai/platform/pricing/).

## Recommended LLM replacements

- For frontier model needs: [`@cf/deepseek-ai/deepseek-v4.1-flash-1017`](/workers-ai/models/deepseek-v4.1-flash-1017/) and [`@cf/zai-org/glm-5.3`](/workers-ai/models/glm-5.3/).
- For smaller model needs: [`@cf/google/gemma-4-26b-a4b-it`](/workers-ai/models/gemma-4-26b-a4b-it/) and [`@cf/qwen/qwen3.8-27b`](/workers-ai/models/qwen3.8-27b/).
