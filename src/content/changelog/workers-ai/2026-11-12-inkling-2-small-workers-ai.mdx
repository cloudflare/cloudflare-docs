[`@cf/thinkingmachines/inkling-2-small`](/workers-ai/models/inkling-2-small/) is now available on Workers AI. Inkling 2 Small is the second generation of Inkling models from Thinking Machines Lab, and the first frontier open model from a US lab on Workers AI.

Inkling 2 Small is more capable than the previous generation. It is a multimodal Mixture-of-Experts model with 594B total parameters and 18B active parameters. Thinking Machines trained it much faster than previous models because of improvements to their pre-training and infrastructure.

Inkling 2 Small works well for:

- **Agentic coding**: Plan, write, and edit code across multi-step tasks.
- **Vision understanding**: Interpret images sent alongside text in the same request.
- **General agentic knowledge work**: Research, analyze, and act on information with tools.

**Key capabilities:**

- **Vision**: Accepts text and image inputs.
- **Reasoning**: Reasoning is on by default at `high` effort. Set `reasoning_effort` to `max`, `medium`, `low`, or `minimal` to change the effort, or to `none` to turn reasoning off.
- **Function calling**: Build agents that invoke tools and APIs across multiple conversation turns.
- **Long context**: A full 1,048,576 token context window.

Inkling 2 Small requires the [Workers Paid plan](/workers/platform/pricing/#workers) or prepaid [AI Gateway credits](/ai-gateway/features/unified-billing/). The default rate limit is 20 requests per minute with standard Workers AI billing and 50 requests per minute with prepaid AI Gateway credits. For details, refer to [limits](/workers-ai/platform/limits/#paid-models).

Use Inkling 2 Small through the [Workers AI binding](/workers-ai/configuration/bindings/) (`env.AI.run()`), the REST API, the [OpenAI-compatible endpoint](/workers-ai/configuration/open-ai-compatibility/), or [AI Gateway](/ai-gateway/).

For more information, refer to the [Inkling 2 Small model page](/workers-ai/models/inkling-2-small/) and [pricing](/workers-ai/platform/pricing/).
