### Interactive Workflow diagrams are now available in the Cloudflare dashboard

Understanding the execution flow of a multi-step [Workflow](/workflows/) can be challenging, especially as your Workflows grow in complexity with loops and conditional branches.

The new **Workflow diagram** on the Workflow overview page provides a visual map of your Workflow's structure, making it easier to:

- Understand how steps connect and execute in sequence
- Visualize loops (with collapsible sections for cleaner views)
- See branching paths for conditional logic

Workflow diagrams are available in beta for all TypeScript Workflows. Check out any Workflow in the [Cloudflare dashboard](https://dash.cloudflare.com/?to=/:account/workers/workflows) to see its diagram.
