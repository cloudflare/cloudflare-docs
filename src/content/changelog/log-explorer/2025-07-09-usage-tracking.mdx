title: Usage tracking
description: Log Explorer customers can now monitor their data ingestion volume
date: 2025-07-09
---

[Log Explorer](/observability/logs/unsampled-security-datasets/) customers can now monitor their data ingestion volume to keep track of their billing. Monthly usage is displayed at the top of the [Log Search](/observability/logs/unsampled-security-datasets/log-search/) and [Manage Datasets](/observability/logs/unsampled-security-datasets/manage-datasets/) screens in Log Explorer.
