We’ve introduced a new Jobs view in the AutoRAG dashboard to help you track and monitor all your indexing activity in one place.

You can now:

- View the status, job ID, source, start/end time, and last sync for each indexing job
- Inspect real-time logs of job events (e.g. `Starting indexing data source...`)
- See a history of past indexing jobs under the Jobs tab

(video here)

This makes it easier to understand what’s happening behind the scenes—and gives you confidence that your data has been indexed and embedded correctly.

Try it out today on the Cloudflare dashboard.
