New [R2](/r2/) product-level **Metrics** page in the Cloudflare dashboard shows bandwidth usage. You can view usage across all buckets or per bucket.

<DashButton url="/?to=/:account/r2/metrics" buttonName="R2 Metrics" />

![R2 upload and download throughput across all buckets over 24 hours](~/assets/images/r2/r2-bandwidth-metrics.png)

Bandwidth throughput is split by object upload and download. The [GraphQL Analytics API](/analytics/graphql-api/) exposes the same bandwidth usage metrics that power the dashboard for your queries and analytics.

For more information, refer to [R2 metrics and analytics](/r2/platform/metrics-analytics/).
