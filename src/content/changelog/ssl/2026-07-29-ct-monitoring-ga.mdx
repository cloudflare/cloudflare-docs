Certificate Transparency Monitoring is now [generally available](https://blog.cloudflare.com/PLACEHOLDER-GA-BLOG-URL/) across all Cloudflare plans.

**What's new:**

- Alerts for certificates Cloudflare issues on your behalf (Universal SSL renewals, backup certificates, Advanced Certificate Manager, Total TLS, SSL for SaaS) are now automatically filtered out.
- Alert emails are clearer and more actionable, with structured certificate details and a direct link to manage CT monitoring settings.

Learn more in the [launch blog post](https://blog.cloudflare.com/PLACEHOLDER-GA-BLOG-URL/) or the [CT Monitoring docs](/ssl/edge-certificates/additional-options/certificate-transparency-monitoring/).
