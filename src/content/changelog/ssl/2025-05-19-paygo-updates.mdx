Updates to Cloudflare for Platforms & Secrets Store free and PAYGO plans:

![Custom origin server](https://developers.cloudflare.com/cloudflare-for-platforms/cloudflare-for-saas/start/advanced-settings/custom-origin/) is now available to Cloudflare for Platforms (previously known as SSL for SaaS) customers on Free, Pro, and Business plans.

!The [limit for custom hostnames](https://developers.cloudflare.com/cloudflare-for-platforms/cloudflare-for-saas/plans/) on a Cloudflare for Platforms PAYGO plan has been raised from 5,000 custom hostnames to 50,000 custom hostnames

![Cloudflare Secrets Store](https://developers.cloudflare.com/secrets-store/) now includes 100 secrets per account in a continuation of the [open beta with Workers integration](https://blog.cloudflare.com/secrets-store-beta/).
