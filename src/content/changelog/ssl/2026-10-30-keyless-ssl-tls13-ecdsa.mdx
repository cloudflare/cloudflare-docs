---
title: Keyless SSL supports TLS 1.3 with ECDSA certificates
description: Keyless SSL connections that use ECDSA certificates negotiate TLS 1.3 when TLS 1.3 is enabled on the zone.
date: 2026-10-30
---

[Keyless SSL](/ssl/keyless-ssl/) supports TLS 1.3 for ECDSA certificates. When [TLS 1.3](/ssl/edge-certificates/additional-options/tls-13/) is enabled on your zone, visitors connecting to a hostname that serves a Keyless ECDSA certificate negotiate TLS 1.3, and your private keys stay on your own key server.

Keyless SSL connections that use RSA certificates continue to negotiate up to TLS 1.2.

For details, refer to the [Keyless SSL limitations](/ssl/keyless-ssl/#limitations).
