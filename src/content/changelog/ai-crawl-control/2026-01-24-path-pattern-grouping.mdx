AI Crawl Control now supports **path pattern grouping** to help you understand which areas of your site AI crawlers target most.

## What's new

### Patterns tab in Most Popular Paths

The **Most popular paths** table now includes a **Patterns** tab that auto-detects URI patterns (`/blog/*`, `/api/v1/*`, `/docs/*`) and groups requests by site area. Instead of seeing hundreds of individual paths, you can now see which areas of your site receive the most crawler attention.

### Destination patterns in Top Referrers

The **Top referrers** section now shows destination patterns alongside referral sources. See which site areas receive the most AI-driven referral traffic, grouped by pattern rather than individual URLs.

Both views are filterable via the top-level filter bar, so you can narrow down by crawler, operator, hostname, or time range.

## Get started

1. Log in to the Cloudflare dashboard and select your account and domain.
2. Go to **AI Crawl Control** > **Metrics**.
3. In **Most popular paths**, select the **Patterns** tab to view grouped site areas.
4. In **Top referrers**, view destination patterns to see which site areas receive AI referral traffic.

Learn more about [analyzing AI traffic](/ai-crawl-control/features/analyze-ai-traffic/).
