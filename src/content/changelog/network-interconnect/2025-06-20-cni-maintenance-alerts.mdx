---
title: CNI maintenance alerts
description: Subscribe to email alerts for planned maintenance on CNI v1 circuits
date: 2025-06-20
---

Customers using Cloudflare Network Interconnect v1 circuits with Cloudflare Virtual Network can subscribe to maintenance alert emails. These alerts notify you of planned maintenance windows that may affect eligible CNI circuits.

For more information, refer to [Monitoring and alerts](/network-interconnect/monitoring-and-alerts/).
