Data loss prevention (DLP) custom pattern entries can use custom Workers to validate matches with organization-specific logic. A Worker can adjust a match's confidence or reject the match.

For setup steps and the Worker contract, refer to [Use Workers to validate DLP pattern matches](/cloudflare-one/data-loss-prevention/detection-entries/custom-validation-workers/).
