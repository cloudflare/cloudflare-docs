title: Cloudflare One adds Periodic Beaconing Traffic to UEBA

date: 2026-11-16
products:
  - cloudflare-one

Cloudflare One adds Periodic Beaconing Traffic as a user and entity behavior analytics (UEBA) risk behavior. It flags a user who contacts the same destination more than 30 times in six hours at steady intervals from 10 seconds to about 12 minutes, when few other users reach it.

Detection uses traffic behavior alone, without threat intelligence, categories, or reputation. Malware can use this pattern to contact a command-and-control (C2) server, but legitimate software also checks in periodically. Treat events as informational leads, not C2 verdicts.

The behavior is off by default and starts at the Info level. Turn it on under **Risk score** > **Risk behaviors**. A continuing beacon is reported once. Events usually arrive 3 to 6 hours after the window closes.

Periodic Beaconing Traffic is available on Enterprise plans.
