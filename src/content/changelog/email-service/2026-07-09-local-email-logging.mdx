---
title: Local email logging now saved to .wrangler directory
description: Email content sent during local development is now logged to the .wrangler/tmp/email directory in your project root for easier inspection.
date: 2026-07-09
---

When [testing email sending locally](/email-service/local-development/sending/), email content (text and HTML) is now saved to `<project root>/.wrangler/tmp/email/<session-id>/` in addition to the system's temporary directory. This makes it easier to locate and inspect sent emails during development without searching through system temporary directories.

The output when sending an email now shows both locations:

```txt
Text: /tmp/miniflare-.../files/email-text/<message-id>.txt
Text (local): <project root>/.wrangler/tmp/email/<session-id>/email-text/<message-id>
```

This change improves the local development experience by providing a predicatble path for email logging, without breaking tools dependent on the original location.
