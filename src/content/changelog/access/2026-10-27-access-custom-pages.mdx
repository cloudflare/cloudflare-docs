Cloudflare Access now supports HTML and Liquid templates for login, identity-denied, forbidden, and command-line approval pages. Use templates to match your organization's branding and give users instructions relevant to the application or request.

Create templates in the Cloudflare One dashboard, validate them in the editor, and assign them to applications or set account defaults. Liquid variables let you display application and request information without maintaining separate pages for each application. Access provides the trusted sign-in and approval controls; your templates do not change authentication or policy decisions.

For supported page types, template examples, and setup instructions, refer to [Access custom pages](/cloudflare-one/reusable-components/custom-pages/access-custom-pages/).
