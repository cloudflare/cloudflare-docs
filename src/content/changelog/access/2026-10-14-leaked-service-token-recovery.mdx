---
title: Rotate leaked Access service tokens before re-enabling them
description: Access requires secret rotation before a service token reported as leaked can be re-enabled.
date: 2026-10-14
products:
  - access
  - cloudflare-one
---

A Cloudflare Access service token reported as leaked cannot be re-enabled until its secret is rotated. This prevents a disabled credential from being put back into use without replacing the exposed secret.

Leaked secrets do not support a rotation grace period. When you rotate one, the previous secret is discarded immediately. Update your services with the new secret, then re-enable the token.

For API rotations, omit `previous_client_secret_expires_at`. A future expiration returns `400`; attempting to re-enable the token before rotation returns `409`.

For recovery steps and leak metadata fields, refer to [Recover a leaked service token](/cloudflare-one/access-controls/service-credentials/service-tokens/#recover-a-leaked-service-token).
