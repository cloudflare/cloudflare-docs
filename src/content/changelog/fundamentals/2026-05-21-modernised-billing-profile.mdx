---
title: New payment options in the Billing Profile
description: You can now add Apple Pay, Google Pay, Link, and Instant Bank Payments via Link to your Billing Profile, with 3D Secure on supported cards.
date: 2026-05-21
---

You can now add Apple Pay, Google Pay, Link, and [Instant Bank Payments via Link](/billing/payment-methods/instant-bank-payments-link/) to your [Billing Profile](/billing/get-started/update-billing-info/), alongside cards and PayPal.

New cards complete 3D Secure authentication when the issuer requires it — for example, the EU under PSD2 and India under RBI.

The Billing Profile and **Pay overdue balances** use the same checkout as product purchases.

The "Backup payment method" label is now "Additional payment method". Auto-retry behavior is unchanged — see [Additional payment method auto-retry](/billing/payment-methods/additional-payment-method-auto-retry/).
