The [Billing Profile](/billing/get-started/update-billing-info/) has a modern UI that unifies billing information, payment method management, and an enhanced subscriptions view under a single **Subscriptions** tab.

## What changed

The **Subscriptions** tab brings billing information, payment method management, and your subscriptions together in one place. The payment management and **Pay overdue balances** flows now use the same checkout as product purchases, so you can pay with Apple Pay, Google Pay, Link, and [Instant Bank Payments via Link](/billing/payment-methods/instant-bank-payments-link/) alongside cards and PayPal.

![Modernized Billing Profile with the Subscriptions tab](~/assets/images/changelog/fundamentals/2026-05-21-modernised-billing-profile.png)

New cards complete 3D Secure authentication when the issuer requires it — for example, the EU under PSD2 and India under RBI.
