You can now pay for Cloudflare services with USDC stablecoin at the checkout. Refer to the [stablecoin payments documentation](/billing/payment-methods/stablecoin-payments/).

## What changed

The payment method picker now includes **Crypto** alongside card, Apple Pay, and Google Pay. Stablecoin payments support one-time charges and recurring billing, including usage-based products like Workers, R2, and Stream.

![Stablecoin payments at checkout](~/assets/images/changelog/fundamentals/2026-05-22-stablecoin-payments.png)

## How to use it

1. At the checkout, select **Crypto** from the payment method options.
2. Connect your wallet (MetaMask, Phantom, Coinbase Wallet, or any WalletConnect-compatible wallet) on the Stripe-hosted page at `crypto.stripe.com`.
3. Sign a one-time smart contract permit to authorize the initial charge and future automatic charges.
4. Your subscription activates after on-chain confirmation, typically within seconds.

## Who is eligible

Stablecoin payments are available to self-serve accounts in eligible regions. USDC is supported on Base and Polygon. If you have a card on file, Cloudflare falls back to it when a stablecoin charge fails.
