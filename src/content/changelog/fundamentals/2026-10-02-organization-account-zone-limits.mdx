title: Organizations support increased account and zone limits
description: Organizations support limits of 20,000 accounts and 200,000 zones across the entire Organization hierarchy.
products:
  - fundamentals

date: 2026-10-02
---

Cloudflare Organizations now support up to **20,000 accounts** and **200,000 zones**. For MSSP/Distributors using sub-organizations, these limits are applied at the root Organization.

Cloudflare can configure different limits for an individual root Organization. The new limits apply to Enterprise and MSSP/Distributor Organizations. Legacy Reseller Partner and Brand Partner Tenants retain their existing quota behavior.

For more information, refer to [Account and zone limits](/fundamentals/organizations/limitations/#account-and-zone-limits).
