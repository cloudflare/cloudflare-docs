title: Organizations support increased account and zone limits

Each root Organization supports up to **20,000 accounts** and **200,000 zones** by default. These limits apply across the entire Organization hierarchy, including sub-organizations.

Cloudflare can configure different limits for an individual root Organization. The new defaults apply to Enterprise and MSSP/Distributor Organizations. Legacy Reseller Partner and Brand Partner Tenants retain their existing quota behavior.

For more information, refer to [Account and zone limits](/fundamentals/organizations/limitations/#account-and-zone-limits).
