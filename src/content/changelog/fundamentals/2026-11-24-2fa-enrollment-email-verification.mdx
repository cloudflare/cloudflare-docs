---
title: Set up two-factor authentication without a password
description: Users who sign in with Apple, GitHub, or Google can now verify their identity by email when enrolling in two-factor authentication.
products:

date: 2026-11-24
---

If you sign in to Cloudflare with Apple, GitHub, or Google, you can now confirm your identity with a code sent to your email when you set up two-factor authentication (2FA). Previously, the dashboard asked for a Cloudflare password, which most social login users never created.

Cloudflare asks you to confirm your identity before changing security settings like 2FA. This protects your account if you leave it signed in on a shared or public computer. That check now offers email verification when you do not have a password, so you no longer need to reset a password you never set just to turn on 2FA.

To set up 2FA, go to **Profile** > **Authentication** and select **Set up** under **Two-Factor Authentication**.

For more information, refer to [Two-factor authentication](/fundamentals/user-profiles/2fa/).
