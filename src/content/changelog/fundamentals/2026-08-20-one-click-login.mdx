We're announcing **One Click Login** for the Cloudflare dashboard. Returning users can now remember accounts on a device and get back into Cloudflare faster without re-entering the same account details on every visit.

**What's New**

**Remember this account on this device**: Users can now choose to remember an account after a successful login. Cloudflare saves the account, login method, and recent sign-in activity for faster access on future visits from the same device.

**Faster sign-in for returning users**: Remembered accounts appear directly on the login page, making it easier to pick a previously used account and continue signing in.

**Works across login methods**: One Click Login supports email and password, Single Sign-On (SSO), and social login, helping streamline sign-in across the different ways users access Cloudflare.

**Built for shared and multi-account workflows**: Users can remember multiple accounts on the same device and remove remembered accounts when they are no longer needed.
