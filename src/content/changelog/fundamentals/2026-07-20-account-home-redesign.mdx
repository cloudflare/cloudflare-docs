Cloudflare has redesigned the Account Home overview page to make it easier to find your resources and get a quick read on your account.

**What's new**

- **Inline search**: A search bar now sits at the top of Account Home. It uses the same search as the Command Palette, with autocomplete, keyboard navigation, and a `⌘K` / `Ctrl+K` shortcut hint.

- **Customizable shortcut columns**: Three columns give you quick access to your Domains, Workers, and Recents, plus a new Storage column that lists your R2, D1, KV, and Postgres & MySQL resources together. You can decide what each column shows and how: pick which columns are visible, show only your starred domains, change the sort order, or choose which products appear in the Storage column. Your choices are saved per account.

- **Clickable column headers and useful empty states**: Column headers link directly to their full list pages. When a column is empty, it now shows a relevant action, such as "Add a domain" or "Ship something new", instead of a blank state.

- **Customizable dashboard**: A new tile grid lets you add, resize, rearrange, and hide the timeseries charts for traffic and security metrics, so you only see the ones that matter to you. Your layout is saved between visits.

- **Onboarding for new accounts**: Accounts without any zones, Workers, Zero Trust organization, or registered domains now see a getting-started view with Build, Domain, and Protect cards that link to the relevant setup flows.

This is just the start. More ways to customize your Account Home are coming soon.

Visit the [Cloudflare dashboard](https://dash.cloudflare.com/) to see the changes.
