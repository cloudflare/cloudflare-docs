We've redesigned the Account Home overview page to make it easier to find your resources and get a quick read on your account.

**What's new**

- **Inline search**: A search bar now sits at the top of Account Home. It uses the same search as the Command Palette, with autocomplete, keyboard navigation, and a `⌘K` / `Ctrl+K` shortcut hint.

- **Shortcut columns**: Three columns give you quick access to your Domains, Workers, and Recents, plus a new Storage column that lists your R2, D1, KV, and Postgres & MySQL resources together. You can filter each column (for example, starred domains only), change the sort order, or choose which products appear, and your choices are saved per account.

- **Clickable column headers and useful empty states**: Column headers link directly to their full list pages. When a column is empty, it now shows a relevant action, such as "Add a domain" or "Ship something new", instead of a blank state.

- **Customizable dashboard**: A new tile grid lets you add, resize, and rearrange timeseries charts for traffic and security metrics. Your layout is saved between visits.

- **Onboarding for new accounts**: Accounts without any zones, Workers, Zero Trust organization, or registered domains now see a getting-started view with Build, Domain, and Protect cards that link to the relevant setup flows.

Visit the [Cloudflare dashboard](https://dash.cloudflare.com/) to see the changes.
