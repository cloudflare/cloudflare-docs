US self-serve customers can now pay for Cloudflare services directly from their bank account using [Instant Bank Payments via Link](/billing/payment-methods/instant-bank-payments-link/).

## What changed

[Link](https://link.co/) is a one-click checkout wallet that remembers your payment details. With this update, Link now supports bank account payments in addition to cards. If you have a bank account saved in Link, it appears as a payment option at checkout. If not, you can connect one during the checkout flow.

## How it works

1. During checkout, Cloudflare presents your saved Link payment methods.
2. Select your bank account from the available options.
3. Confirm the payment.

Your bank account is available for future purchases without re-entering details after your first Link authentication.

## Who is eligible

Instant Bank Payments via Link is available to US-based self-serve accounts across all Cloudflare products. Your existing cards remain available at checkout.

## Identifying bank payments

Bank-based Link payments appear in your billing history with the payment method shown as `link` and last four digits as `0000`. Card-based Link payments display your card's actual last four digits.

For more information, refer to the [Instant Bank Payments via Link documentation](/billing/payment-methods/instant-bank-payments-link/).
