[AI Search](/ai-search/) keyword search with the `trigram` tokenizer now matches Japanese, Chinese, and Korean queries, including full natural-language questions such as `経費精算の締め日はいつですか？`. Previously, a query without spaces was treated as a single exact phrase and usually returned no keyword results.

The `porter` tokenizer does not support Japanese. If your content is in Japanese, set `keyword_tokenizer` to `trigram`. Changing the tokenizer triggers a full reindex.

Learn more about [keyword search](/ai-search/configuration/indexing/keyword-search/#keyword-tokenizer).
