Enterprise Bot Management customers have two new visibility tools:

- [**BotBase**](/bots/botbase/): a searchable directory of all bots and agents Cloudflare tracks, showing each bot's behavior classification and detection ID so you can filter traffic and target a specific bot in [Security rules](/security/rules/).
- [**Attribution Business Insights**](/bots/attribution-business-insights/): a dashboard showing site-wide and per-operator crawl-to-referral ratios alongside the bot traffic reaching your content.
