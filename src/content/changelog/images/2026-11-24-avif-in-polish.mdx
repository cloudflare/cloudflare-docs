title: Automatic AVIF conversion with Polish
description: Polish can now convert JPEG and PNG origin images to AVIF and serve the smaller format to compatible browsers.

---

[Cloudflare Polish](/images/polish/) can now create AVIF versions of JPEG and PNG source images, alongside existing WebP support. AVIF often produces smaller files than WebP, reducing transfer sizes and improving load times for image-heavy sites such as product catalogs, photo galleries, and news publications.

Polish automatically optimizes images on your zone that are served from your origin server. It strips metadata, applies compression, and transcodes JPEG and PNG files to more efficient image formats.

[Activate Polish](/images/polish/activate-polish/) with AVIF and WebP through the Cloudflare CLI, REST API, or dashboard. With the CLI, run:

```sh
cf zones settings edit polish --zone example.com --body '{"value":"lossy"}'
cf zones settings edit avif --zone example.com --body '{"value":"on"}'
cf zones settings edit webp --zone example.com --body '{"value":"on"}'
```

AVIF and WebP can be enabled at the same time. When both formats are enabled, Polish transcodes JPEG and PNG files based on browser support:

- The browser must advertise AVIF support in its `Accept` header.
- Polish serves AVIF only if the converted image is smaller than the original.
- If the browser supports both AVIF and WebP, Polish prefers AVIF.
- Browsers without AVIF support continue to receive WebP or the optimized original format.

Polish skips AVIF conversion when the result would be larger, the source image is animated or smaller than 16 by 16 pixels, or AVIF capacity is temporarily unavailable. During periods of high demand, Polish may also use faster compression settings to maintain response times while still serving only AVIF files that are smaller than the original.

For format selection and fallback behavior, refer to [Compression and format selection](/images/polish/compression-and-format-selection/) and [AVIF is not served](/images/polish/troubleshooting/avif-not-served/).
