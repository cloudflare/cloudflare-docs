title: View delivery analytics for hosted images
description: View up to 30 days of delivery analytics for assets stored in Images, including traffic sources, response status codes, formats, and variants.
date: 2026-11-03
---

[Images](/images/) lets you store, optimize, and deliver hosted images through a scalable image pipeline. The new [Images Analytics dashboard](https://dash.cloudflare.com/?to=/:account/images/hosted) provides up to 30 days of delivery data for assets stored in Images, helping you understand how and where they are requested.

![Hosted images analytics showing request totals, countries, and response status codes](~/assets/images/images/hosted-analytics.png)

In **Images & Stream** > **Hosted images** > **Analytics**, you can:

- **Investigate traffic trends and sources.** Review request volume over time, then filter by country, autonomous system number (ASN), or referrer to trace spikes and unexpected traffic.
- **Troubleshoot delivery.** Break down requests by response status code to identify errors. Filter by browser or operating system to understand which output formats different clients receive.
- **Understand asset and variant usage.** Find the most-requested source images and compare predefined variants with inline configurations to see which configurations account for the most requests.

Open the [Images Analytics dashboard](https://dash.cloudflare.com/?to=/:account/images/hosted) to investigate recent traffic changes and identify the hosted images and variants driving requests.
