Cloudflare coordinates routine CNI-disruptive maintenance across resilient Cloudflare Network Interconnect (CNI) deployments, including across CNI locations.

## Planned maintenance

Routine CNI-disruptive maintenance is planned work that can interrupt traffic on an affected CNI connection. Cloudflare schedules this work to preserve the remaining connections in your resilient deployment.

| CNI deployment                                                                                                   | During routine CNI-disruptive maintenance |
| ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------- |
| One CNI connection at one location                                                                               | The connection can be interrupted.        |
| Two CNI connections on separate devices at one location                                                          | One connection remains in service.        |
| Four CNI connections across two coordinated locations, with two connections on separate devices at each location | Three connections remain in service.      |

## Emergency and non-routine maintenance

Non-routine maintenance, such as maintenance that affects an entire PoP, can affect all CNI connections at the affected location. Cloudflare avoids performing non-routine maintenance at multiple coordinated locations at the same time.

Emergency maintenance can require shorter notice. Cloudflare coordinates emergency maintenance across locations where feasible.

## Receive maintenance notifications

To configure circuit-specific or point-of-presence (PoP) maintenance notifications, refer to [Monitoring and alerts](/network-interconnect/monitoring-and-alerts/).
