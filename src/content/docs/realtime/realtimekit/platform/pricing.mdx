RealtimeKit usage is charged according to the pricing model below:

:::note

RealtimeKit participant and export usage is measured in minutes. In contrast, Realtime SFU, MoQ Relay, and TURN usage is measured in GB of egress. RealtimeKit transcription uses Workers AI pricing.

To estimate your RealtimeKit costs, refer to the [RealtimeKit pricing calculator](https://realtime.cloudflare.com/#pricing-calculator).

:::

| Feature                                               | Price                                 |
| ----------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------- |
| Audio/Video Participant                               | $0.002 / minute                       |
| Audio-Only Participant                                | $0.0005 / minute                      |
| Export (recording, RTMP or HLS streaming)             | $0.010 / minute                       |
| Export (recording, RTMP or HLS streaming, audio only) | $0.003 / minute                       |
| Export (Raw RTP) into R2                              | $0.0005 / minute                      |
| Transcription (Real-time)                             | Standard model pricing via Workers AI |

Whether a participant is an audio-only participant or an audio/video participant is determined by the `Meeting Type` of their [preset](/realtime/realtimekit/concepts/preset/).

All accounts, including Free and Enterprise accounts, receive 10,000 audio/video participant minutes and 10,000 audio-only participant minutes free each month.
