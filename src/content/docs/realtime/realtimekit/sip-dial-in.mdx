A phone caller can join a live RealtimeKit meeting as an audio participant. Your voice provider connects a phone number to the meeting's Session Initiation Protocol (SIP) address. RealtimeKit answers the call, adds the participant, and connects the audio. The caller can use an ordinary phone.

## Enable dial-in

You need a [RealtimeKit app and API token](/realtime/realtimekit/quickstart/) with **Realtime** or **Realtime Admin** permission. Use the meeting API from your backend. A meeting response can contain the SIP password, so keep the response out of browser clients and logs.

<Steps>

1. To create a meeting with dial-in, set `enable_sip` to `true`.

   <APIRequest
     method="POST"
     path="/accounts/{account_id}/realtime/kit/{app_id}/meetings"
     json={{ title: "Support room", enable_sip: true }}
     roles={false}
   />

   For an existing meeting, use this update instead. The meeting replacement operation (`PUT`) ignores `enable_sip`.

   <APIRequest
     method="PATCH"
     path="/accounts/{account_id}/realtime/kit/{app_id}/meetings/{meeting_id}"
     json={{ enable_sip: true }}
     roles={false}
   />

2. In the response, save the meeting ID and the three fields in `data.sip`. You can also read them with an authenticated `GET` on the meeting. When dial-in is off, the meeting returns `sip: null`.

   | Field | Where it goes |
   | --- | --- |
   | `sip_uri` | The destination your provider dials for this meeting |
   | `username` | The provider's SIP authentication username |
   | `password` | The provider's SIP authentication password |

3. Configure your voice provider to route a number to this SIP address. For Twilio Programmable Voice, follow [Route a Twilio number to Cloudflare](/realtime/sfu/features/sip/twilio/). Give callers the provider's phone number. RealtimeKit handles the SIP webhooks and SFU tracks for this meeting.

</Steps>

## Test a call

<Steps>

1. Join the meeting from a browser or mobile client using the [RealtimeKit quickstart](/realtime/realtimekit/quickstart/). Turn on the microphone **before dialing**. Creating a meeting reserves the room. A phone caller needs a [live session](/realtime/realtimekit/concepts/session-lifecycle/) to join it.
2. Call the provider's number from a phone. Look for a new audio participant in the meeting. Its display name comes from the user part of the SIP caller address, often a phone number. The fallback name is `Phone caller`.
3. Speak in each direction and confirm both participants hear each other. Hang up the phone and confirm the caller leaves. A moderator can also remove the caller or end the session.

</Steps>

## What participants can hear

The phone caller joins with the reserved `sip_preset`, which allows microphone audio and skips the waiting room. Anyone who can call the example Twilio number can reach that live meeting. If you require a PIN or caller screening, configure it at the provider before forwarding the call. Meeting clients receive the caller's audio as a participant track.

When answering, RealtimeKit selects up to 32 unmuted meeting microphone tracks. Screen-share audio is excluded. That selection lasts for the call. If someone joins or publishes a new microphone later, have the phone caller redial to include that microphone.

## Troubleshoot dial-in

| Symptom                                               | What to do                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| ----------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| The provider receives SIP `480`                       | Have a participant join the meeting, then dial again. The phone caller cannot start a session.                                                                                                        |
| The meeting hears the caller, but the phone is silent | Make sure a meeting participant has an active microphone, then redial. A call can connect with an empty audio mix.                                                                                    |
| The call ends after other people leave                | RealtimeKit ends the call after about 60 seconds without another eligible participant. Other phone callers, recording or livestream participants, and people in the waiting room do not keep it open. |
| The provider repeatedly receives SIP `401`            | Read the current meeting credentials and update the provider. Check that the number points to this meeting's full SIP URI.                                                                            |

## Turn off dial-in

Send `{"enable_sip":false}` with `PATCH` to the same meeting URL. This removes its dial-in configuration. To disconnect a caller already in the meeting, remove that participant. Enabling dial-in again creates a new SIP address and credentials. Update your provider before sharing the number again.

For an application that makes its own accept decisions or changes the audio mix during a call, use [Realtime SFU SIP dial-in](/realtime/sfu/features/sip/).
