## Renew custom certificates

Since Cloudflare cannot renew uploaded certificates, you should ensure that you replace or [update](/ssl/edge-certificates/custom-certificates/uploading/#update-or-renew-an-existing-custom-certificate) an expiring custom certificate before it expires, otherwise your visitors may not be able to connect.

Cloudflare automatically sends email notifications 30 and 14 days before your custom certificate expires. The email is sent to users who have the SSL/TLS, Administrator, or Super Administrator [roles](/fundamentals/manage-members/roles/).

These notifications are reminders only. Cloudflare cannot renew an uploaded certificate for you — to maintain coverage, upload a replacement before the current certificate expires.

:::note

When renewing a custom certificate, you can reuse a [previously generated CSR](/ssl/edge-certificates/additional-options/certificate-signing-requests/).

If you are on an Enterprise plan and want to renew a custom (modern) certificate, consider requesting access to [Staging environment (Beta)](/ssl/edge-certificates/staging-environment/).

Renewing a certificate also rebuilds its certificate chain using Cloudflare's current trust store. Refer to [How Cloudflare builds the certificate chain](/ssl/edge-certificates/custom-certificates/bundling-methodologies/#how-cloudflare-builds-the-certificate-chain).
:::

## Expired certificates

If another active certificate - custom, [advanced](/ssl/edge-certificates/advanced-certificate-manager/), or [universal](/ssl/edge-certificates/universal-ssl/) - already covers some or all of the <GlossaryTooltip term="Subject Alternative Names (SANs)">SANs</GlossaryTooltip> in the expiring custom certificate, Cloudflare may remove the expiring custom certificate up to 24 hours before its expiration date (`Not After`, in UTC). There is no expected downtime due to certificate transition.

If no other certificate covers these hostnames, Cloudflare keeps the custom certificate until it expires, and then removes it.

:::note
This 24-hour window is an automated fallback, not a deadline for renewal. You can [update your custom certificate](/ssl/edge-certificates/custom-certificates/uploading/#update-or-renew-an-existing-custom-certificate) as soon as your CA issues the renewed certificate.

If the fallback certificate is not the one you want served, update your custom certificate before this window starts.
:::

Affected domains and subdomains will fall back to any other active certificate covering the hostnames on the expiring certificate.

:::caution

All certificates in a [certificate pack](/ssl/edge-certificates/custom-certificates/#certificate-packs) are treated as one object.
The expiration date of a certificate pack is equivalent to the soonest `Not After` date among the certificates in the pack.

For example if you have a custom certificate made of an ECSDA and a RSA certificate, if one of them expires the whole pack will be removed.
:::

## Migrate to other certificate types

If you no longer want to use your custom certificate but still want your website or application to be covered with SSL/TLS, you can do the following:

1. In the Cloudflare dashboard, go to the **Edge Certificates** page.

   <DashButton url="/?to=/:account/:zone/ssl-tls/edge-certificates" />

2. Make sure there is already an active [universal](/ssl/edge-certificates/universal-ssl/) or [advanced](/ssl/edge-certificates/advanced-certificate-manager/) certificate covering the same hostnames.
3. Delete your custom certificate.
