Contracted enterprise customers can try certain products and features for 30 days.

Once you enable a product or feature, it is not an officially contracted service until you purchase it. Cloudflare will reach out with questions, feedback, and a sales quote if you want to continue using the product.

To cancel, contact your sales team before the 30-day period ends.

## Eligibility

Not all enterprise accounts qualify for preview services. Eligibility depends on your account type and contract details.

The following account types are **not eligible** for self-service preview:

- Partner accounts
- Accounts with purchase order (PO) based renewals
- Accounts flagged for collectibility
- Accounts with self-service purchasing disabled

If your account is not eligible, the **Enable** button does not appear on the product's dashboard page. Instead, you may see a purchase flow, a "Contact your Account Manager" message, or another prompt depending on the product. Contact your account team to request access.

:::note

If you are on a partner account, contact your partner or Cloudflare account team to enable preview services.

:::

## Recommendations

Since these services are not yet part of your contract, use them in staging or other non-production environments.

## View enabled products

To view which products you have previously enabled, go to your [Account Subscriptions](https://dash.cloudflare.com/?to=/:account/billing/subscriptions) page and look for items with **Terms** of **NOT IN CONTRACT**.
