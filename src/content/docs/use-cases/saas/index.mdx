Build multi-tenant platforms with custom domains, isolated compute, and per-customer configuration.

## Overview

SaaS platforms face unique challenges: supporting custom domains for customers, isolating tenant data and compute, and scaling infrastructure as your customer base grows. Cloudflare for Platforms provides the building blocks to extend Cloudflare's capabilities to your own customers.

### Common challenges

- **Custom domains** - Customers want to use their own domains with your service
- **SSL certificates** - Managing certificates for thousands of customer domains
- **Tenant isolation** - Keeping customer data and compute separate
- **Per-tenant configuration** - Different settings, rules, and limits per customer
- **White-labeling** - Allowing customers to brand the experience as their own

## Architecture patterns

### Custom domains for SaaS

Allow customers to use their own domains with your platform:

1. **SSL for SaaS** provisions certificates automatically when customers add domains
2. **Custom Hostnames** route traffic from customer domains to your application
3. **Workers** applies per-tenant logic based on the incoming hostname

### Programmable platforms

Let customers extend your platform with their own code:

1. **Workers for Platforms** provides isolated execution environments per tenant
2. **Dispatch namespaces** route requests to the correct customer Worker
3. **Tail Workers** capture logs and analytics across all tenant code

### Multi-tenant data architecture

Isolate and manage data across customers:

1. **D1** databases per tenant for complete data isolation
2. **Durable Objects** coordinate real-time state within tenant boundaries
3. **R2** buckets with per-tenant prefixes or separate buckets

## Next steps

After setting up SSL for SaaS, consider these additional capabilities:

### Workers for Platforms

Let your customers deploy their own code on your platform:

1. [Create a dispatch namespace](/cloudflare-for-platforms/workers-for-platforms/get-started/configuration/#1-create-a-dispatch-namespace)
2. [Upload customer Workers](/cloudflare-for-platforms/workers-for-platforms/get-started/configuration/#2-upload-a-user-worker)
3. [Route requests to customer Workers](/cloudflare-for-platforms/workers-for-platforms/get-started/configuration/#4-route-to-the-user-worker)

For the complete guide, refer to [Workers for Platforms get started](/cloudflare-for-platforms/workers-for-platforms/get-started/).

### Per-tenant data isolation

Store customer data with appropriate isolation:

- **D1** - Create separate databases per tenant for complete isolation. Refer to [D1 get started](/d1/get-started/).
- **R2** - Use bucket prefixes or separate buckets per tenant. Refer to [R2 get started](/r2/get-started/).
- **Durable Objects** - Coordinate real-time state within tenant boundaries. Refer to [Durable Objects get started](/durable-objects/get-started/).
