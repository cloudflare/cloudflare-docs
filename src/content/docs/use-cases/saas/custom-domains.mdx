## Features

- **Custom domains** (Cloudflare for Platforms) - Customers bring their own domains and have them route to your platform with per-tenant configuration
- **Automatic SSL** (SSL for SaaS) - Certificates provisioned and renewed automatically for every customer domain with no manual steps
- **No customer action required** (SSL for SaaS) - Cloudflare can complete domain validation without requiring action from the customer
- **Scale** (SSL for SaaS) - Support thousands of custom hostnames per domain without additional per-hostname infrastructure
