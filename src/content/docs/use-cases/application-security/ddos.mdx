## What you get

- **Always-on protection** (DDoS Protection) - Attacks are mitigated automatically with no manual intervention required
- **Unlimited mitigation** (DDoS Protection) - No caps on attack size or duration
- **Layer 3/4 protection** (DDoS Protection) - Block network-layer floods and amplification attacks at the network edge
- **Customizable sensitivity** (Advanced DDoS) - Tune protection thresholds and actions to match your traffic patterns
