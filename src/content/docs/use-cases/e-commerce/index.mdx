E-commerce applications require exceptional performance, security, and reliability. Cloudflare provides a comprehensive platform to protect and accelerate your online store.

<DirectoryListing />

## Prerequisites

- A [Cloudflare account](https://dash.cloudflare.com/sign-up).
- A domain [added to Cloudflare](/fundamentals/manage-domains/add-site/). All products in this use case — SSL/TLS (Transport Layer Security), DDoS (Distributed Denial of Service) Protection, WAF (Web Application Firewall), Bot security, Cache, Images, Load Balancing, and Waiting Room — require your domain to be onboarded to Cloudflare with DNS records proxied through Cloudflare's network.
- If your store is hosted on a SaaS platform that already uses Cloudflare — such as [Shopify](/cloudflare-for-platforms/cloudflare-for-saas/saas-customers/provider-guides/shopify/), [BigCommerce](/cloudflare-for-platforms/cloudflare-for-saas/saas-customers/provider-guides/bigcommerce/), or [Salesforce Commerce Cloud](/cloudflare-for-platforms/cloudflare-for-saas/saas-customers/provider-guides/salesforce-commerce-cloud/) — follow the setup steps in the provider guide for your platform to add your own Cloudflare zone on top of your provider's existing configuration.
