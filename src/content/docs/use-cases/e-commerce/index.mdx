E-commerce applications require exceptional performance, security, and reliability. Cloudflare provides a comprehensive platform to protect and accelerate your online store.

<DirectoryListing />

## Prerequisites

- A [Cloudflare account](https://dash.cloudflare.com/sign-up).
- A domain [added to Cloudflare](/fundamentals/manage-domains/add-site/). All products in this use case — DNS (Domain Name System), SSL/TLS (Transport Layer Security), DDoS (Distributed Denial of Service) Protection, WAF (Web Application Firewall), Bot security, Cache, Images, Load Balancing, and Waiting Room — require your domain to be onboarded to Cloudflare with DNS records proxied through Cloudflare's network.
- If you do not yet have a domain, you can [register one through Cloudflare Registrar](/registrar/get-started/register-domain/) at cost price with no markup fees.
