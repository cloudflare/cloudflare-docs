## Recover your account

If you lose access to your Cloudflare account due to forgotten credentials, revoked sessions, or unavailable administrators, you can take the steps below to regain access while maintaining account security.

### Common recovery scenarios

Account recovery may be required if:

- You no longer have access to the email address associated with your account
- All active sessions were revoked
- The only Super Administrator is unavailable
- You are locked out due to authentication issues

### Recovery options

#### Recover access using email

If you still have access to your account email address:

1. Go to the Cloudflare login page.
2. Select **Forgot your password?**
3. Follow the password reset instructions sent to your email.

#### Recover access without email access

If you no longer have access to the email address on the account:

- Ensure another **Super Administrator** exists who can update your email address or role.
- If no Super Administrator is available, contact Cloudflare Support and provide proof of account ownership.

#### Recover from administrative lockout

To prevent lockout scenarios:

- Maintain at least two active Super Administrators on the account.
- Ensure all Super Administrators have verified email addresses.
- Regularly review active sessions and account members.

### Contact Cloudflare Support

If self-service recovery is not possible, submit a support request with the following information:

- Account name or account ID
- Affected email address
- Description of the access issue
- Any relevant verification details requested by Support

### Outcome

Account access is restored or escalated through Cloudflare Support, ensuring secure reestablishment of administrative control.
