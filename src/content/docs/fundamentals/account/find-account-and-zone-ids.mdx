Once you [set up a new account](/fundamentals/account/) and [add your domain](/fundamentals/manage-domains/add-site/) to Cloudflare, you may need access to your zone and account IDs for API operations.

## Copy your account ID

<Steps>
1. Open the Cloudflare dashboard.

   <DashButton url="/?to=/:account/home" />

2. Select **Search** (or press `CMD/CTRL + K` from any page).
3. Enter `Copy account ID` and select the result to copy.

   ![Search results showing the Copy account ID command](~/assets/images/fundamentals/get-started/quick-search-account-id.png)
</Steps>

## Copy your zone ID

<Steps>
1. In the Cloudflare dashboard, select the domain you want the zone ID for.

   <DashButton url="/?to=/:account/domains/overview" />

2. On the domain **Overview** page, locate the **API** section towards the bottom of the page.

   ![Domain Overview page with the API section highlighted](~/assets/images/fundamentals/get-started/dash-overview-api-highlighted.png)

3. Select the copy button next to **Zone ID**. You can also copy **Account ID** from this section.
</Steps>

## Find account ID in Workers & Pages

You can also find your account ID from the **Workers & Pages** section of your account.

<Steps>
1. In the Cloudflare dashboard, go to **Workers & Pages**.

   <DashButton url="/?to=/:account/workers-and-pages" />

2. Locate the **Account Details** section.
3. Select the copy button next to **Account ID**.

   ![Workers & Pages Overview page with the Account ID section highlighted](~/assets/images/fundamentals/get-started/workers-account-id.png)
</Steps>
