Email Service is a zone-level feature. By default, Email Sending and Email Routing apply to the apex domain (for example, `example.com`). You can extend either service to subdomains of the same zone, such as `mail.example.com` or `corp.example.com`.

## Add a subdomain to Email Routing

1. Log in to the [Cloudflare dashboard](https://dash.cloudflare.com/) and select your account and domain.
2. Go to **Compute** > **Email Service** > **Email Routing**.

   <DashButton url="/?to=/:account/email-service/routing" />

3. Select the apex domain, then open **Settings**.
4. Under **Subdomains**, enter the subdomain you want to enable in the inline form and submit it.

Cloudflare adds the required DNS records to the subdomain. Once the records propagate, you can create [routing rules](/email-service/configuration/email-routing-addresses/) on the subdomain in the same way as on the apex domain.

## Add a subdomain to Email Sending

Email Sending treats a subdomain as a separate sending domain. Onboard the subdomain through the standard onboarding flow:

1. Log in to the [Cloudflare dashboard](https://dash.cloudflare.com/) and select your account.
2. Go to **Compute** > **Email Service** > **Email Sending**.

   <DashButton url="/?to=/:account/email-service/sending" />

3. Select **Onboard Domain** and choose the subdomain you want to send from. The onboarding flow adds the `cf-bounce` MX, SPF, DKIM, and DMARC records to the subdomain.
4. Select **Done**.

Once verified, you can send emails from addresses on the subdomain (for example, `notifications@mail.example.com`) using either the [REST API](/email-service/api/send-emails/rest-api/) or the [Workers binding](/email-service/api/send-emails/workers-api/).
