Email Service writes configuration changes to [Cloudflare audit logs](/fundamentals/account/account-security/review-audit-logs/). Use audit logs to track who changed what and when.

The following Email Routing actions are recorded:

- Add or edit a routing rule.
- Add a destination address.
- Change the status of an address (for example, from pending to verified).
- Enable, disable, or unlock the zone for Email Routing.

To review audit logs, refer to [Review audit logs](/fundamentals/account/account-security/review-audit-logs/).
