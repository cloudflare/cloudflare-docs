Cloudflare Email Service implements sending limits to prevent abuse, ensure service quality, and maintain high deliverability for all users. These limits automatically adjust based on your account type and sending reputation.

<Sandboxing />

## Default limits

Your account starts with conservative limits for sending emails to arbitrary emails. These increase as you demonstrate good sending practices:

| Account Type                                  | Daily  | Hourly | Monthly |
| --------------------------------------------- | ------ | ------ | ------- |
| **Paid account with < 2 months of age**       | 100    | 10     | 10,000  |
| **Paid account with > 2 months of age**       | 1,000  | 100    | 10,000  |
| **Paid account with production access limit** | 10,000 | 10,000 | 10,000  |

## Request higher limits

If you need higher limits for production use or have outgrown the default quotas, contact [Cloudflare Support](https://dash.cloudflare.com/support) or your account team to request an increase.

## Compliance

All email sending must follow applicable anti-spam laws and regulations to maintain good standing and deliverability.

- **CAN-SPAM Act** (United States)
- **GDPR** (European Union)
- **CASL** (Canada)
- Include proper unsubscribe mechanisms
- Honor opt-out requests promptly
