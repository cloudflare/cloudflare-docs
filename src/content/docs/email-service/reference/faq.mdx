Common questions about Cloudflare Email Service.

## Limits and Usage

### Why are limits so conservative initially?

Conservative initial limits help us:

- Prevent abuse and spam
- Maintain high deliverability for all users
- Comply with ISP and regulatory requirements
- Build trust with email providers

### How long does the review process take?

We aim to respond to all limit increase requests within 24 hours during business days. Complex requests may require additional review time.

### Can limits be decreased?

Yes, limits may be decreased if:

- Account shows patterns of abuse
- Compliance violations are detected
- Sending practices harm deliverability
- Account becomes inactive

### What happens if I exceed my limits?

- Emails may be queued or rejected
- You'll receive error responses with rate limit information
- Repeated violations may result in service restrictions
- We recommend monitoring usage proactively

### What is sender reputation?

Sender reputation is a score based on:

- Email authentication setup
- Bounce and complaint rates
- Sending volume patterns
- Recipient engagement
- Domain and IP history

### Can I use this for marketing emails?

Email Service is primarily designed for transactional emails.

### Where can I report abuse or spam?

Report abuse to: abuse@cloudflare.com

Include:

- Full email headers
- Description of the issue
- Any relevant account information
