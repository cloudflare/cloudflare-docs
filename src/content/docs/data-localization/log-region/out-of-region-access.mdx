With the default log region configuration, users who are physically located outside the configured log region will not have access to view analytics on the dashboard or retrieve data through the standard API endpoint. When **Allow out-of-region access** is enabled, your logs will still be stored exclusively within the configured region but will be made available to authorized users on your account regardless of their physical location.

This is useful when your operations, security, or engineering teams are distributed across multiple regions and need visibility into traffic analytics without relocating the underlying data.

For example, when **Allow out-of-region access** is **disabled** on an account with a US log region, users in Europe will not be able to see any analytics or logs on the dashboard.

When **Allow out-of-region access** is enabled on an account with a US log region, users in both Europe and the US will be able to see analytics on the dashboard even though the logs are stored exclusively in the US.
