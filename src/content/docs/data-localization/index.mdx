Cloudflare data residency lets you choose where your data is processed and stored, while keeping the security and performance benefits of the global network. Organizations subject to data residency regulations such as [GDPR](https://www.cloudflare.com/trust-hub/gdpr/) can control where TLS private keys are stored, where logs and analytics are kept, where stored content resides, and where HTTPS traffic is decrypted and processed.

These controls are sold as the Data Localization Suite (DLS).

New here? Start with [What stays in your region](/data-localization/what-stays-in-your-region/), which lists the four settings, the regions each one supports, and every product exception on one page.

---

## Settings

<Feature header="Processing region" href="/data-localization/regional-services/">

Choose which Cloudflare data centers are allowed to decrypt and inspect your HTTPS traffic. Configured with Regional Services.

</Feature>

<Feature header="Storage region" href="/data-localization/what-stays-in-your-region/#3-no-storage-region-yet">

Choose where content you store resides. Configured per product with jurisdictional restrictions in [R2](/r2/reference/data-location/) and [Durable Objects](/durable-objects/reference/data-location/).

</Feature>

<Feature header="Log region" href="/data-localization/metadata-boundary/">

Keep logs and analytics that could identify your end users in the region you selected. Configured with the Customer Metadata Boundary.

</Feature>

<Feature header="Key region" href="/data-localization/geo-key-manager/">

Restrict where your TLS private keys are stored. Configured with Geo Key Manager.

</Feature>
