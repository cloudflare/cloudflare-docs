The Data Localization Suite (DLS) lets you choose where Cloudflare decrypts and processes your traffic, where your logs are stored, and which data centers can use your TLS private keys, while keeping the security and performance benefits of the global network. Organizations subject to data residency regulations such as [GDPR](https://www.cloudflare.com/trust-hub/gdpr/) can use DLS alongside product storage jurisdictions to keep each kind of data in a region.

Each control covers one kind of data and has its own region list. Start with the kind of data you need to keep in region:

| What you need to keep in region                                | Control                                                             | Regions                                     |
| -------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------- |
| Traffic being decrypted, inspected, and processed              | [Regional Services](/data-localization/regional-services/)          | 35 regions, or a custom set of data centers |
| Content you store, such as R2 objects and Durable Object state | Storage jurisdictions, set per resource in each product             | EU, US, or FedRAMP                          |
| Logs and analytics about your traffic                          | [Customer Metadata Boundary](/data-localization/metadata-boundary/) | EU or US                                    |
| Use of your TLS private keys                                   | [Geo Key Manager](/data-localization/geo-key-manager/)              | 11 regions                                  |

No single control covers all four. Regional Services does not affect where logs or stored content reside, and Customer Metadata Boundary covers logs only, not stored content.

To check each product in your architecture, refer to [Product compatibility](/data-localization/compatibility/). For the full region list per control, refer to [Region support](/data-localization/region-support/). To see the controls applied to your account, open the [Data Localization Suite overview](https://dash.cloudflare.com/?to=/:account/dls/overview) in the dashboard.

## Features

<Feature header="Regional Services" href="/data-localization/regional-services/">

Choose which Cloudflare data centers can decrypt and process your HTTPS traffic.

</Feature>

<Feature header="Customer Metadata Boundary" href="/data-localization/metadata-boundary/">

Keep logs and analytics about your traffic, which could identify your end users, in the EU or the US.

</Feature>

<Feature header="Geo Key Manager" href="/data-localization/geo-key-manager/">

Choose which data centers can use your TLS private keys to decrypt traffic.

</Feature>
