Cloudflare can keep four kinds of data in a region you choose. Each setting has its own region list, so check that first: it is the most common surprise.

| What you are choosing                       | Setting                                                    | Regions available                                                                      |
| ------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Traffic being decrypted and inspected       | [Processing region](/data-localization/processing-region/) | 35 regions, or a custom list of data centers. Countries, plus FedRAMP, IRAP, and NATO. |
| Content you store in R2 and Durable Objects | [Storage region](/data-localization/storage-region/)       | EU, US, or FedRAMP only                                                                |
| Logs and analytics                          | [Log region](/data-localization/log-region/)               | EU or US only                                                                          |
| TLS private keys                            | [Key region](/data-localization/key-region/)               | 11 regions                                                                             |

For the full region list per setting, refer to [Regions by setting](/data-localization/regions-by-setting/). If your contract or the dashboard uses different names, refer to [names you may see elsewhere](/data-localization/#names-you-may-see-elsewhere).

<Aside type="caution" title="Outside the EU and the US">

Cloudflare can decrypt and inspect traffic inside your country, but your logs and stored content will reside in the EU or the US. There is no exception today.

</Aside>

If a product is not listed below, it is in scope. These three lists cover every exception.

## 1. Not available with data residency

- [Argo Smart Routing](/argo-smart-routing/)
- [Onion Routing](/network/onion-routing/)
- [O2O](/cloudflare-for-platforms/cloudflare-for-saas/saas-customers/how-it-works/)
- [Trace](/rules/trace-request/)
- HTTP/3 (QUIC)
- [Encrypted Client Hello](/ssl/edge-certificates/ech/)
- [ICMP](https://www.cloudflare.com/learning/ddos/glossary/internet-control-message-protocol-icmp/)
- [Smart Placement](/workers/configuration/smart-placement/)
- [Vectorize](/vectorize/)
- Smart Tiered Caching
- [Network Error Logging](/fundamentals/reference/network-error-logging/)
- [Web Analytics and Real User Monitoring (RUM)](/web-analytics/)
- [CASB](/cloudflare-one/applications/casb/)
- [Log Explorer](/log-explorer/)
- `workers.dev` and `pages.dev`

These require Cloudflare to choose a location for you, or route around the region you set.

For `workers.dev` and `pages.dev`, use your own domain. Regular and Custom [Tiered Cache](/cache/how-to/tiered-cache/) work; only Smart Tiered Caching does not. [Workers KV](/kv/), [Workers AI](/workers-ai/) and [Stream](/stream/) run outside your processing region.

## 2. Works, with something missing

| What you lose                                    | Affects                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |

| Not regionalized at all                          | Gateway DNS filtering, [Access JWTs](/cloudflare-one/access-controls/applications/http-apps/authorization-cookie/validating-json/), [Turnstile](/turnstile/) Siteverify API                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| Unavailable                                      | Gateway [File Sandboxing](/cloudflare-one/traffic-policies/http-policies/file-sandboxing/)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| Bypasses your processing region                  | DNS [outgoing zone transfers](/dns/zone-setups/zone-transfers/cloudflare-as-primary/), which carry global IPs                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |

A [regional hostname](/data-localization/processing-region/regional-hostnames/) does not regionalize a Spectrum application on the same hostname.                                                                                                                                                                                                   |

## 3. No storage region yet

- [D1](/d1/)
- [Workers KV](/kv/)
- [Images](/images/)
- [AI Gateway](/ai-gateway/observability/logging/) logs

Logs for these products stay in your log region. The stored objects have no region setting today.

## Before you start

**Bring your own domain.** Cloudflare-managed hostnames and storage are never regionalized. Developer platform products regionalize through a custom domain assigned to a region. R2, Images, Pages, Stream and Workers stay global until you set one.

**An empty dashboard does not mean your data left the region.** With an EU log region, many dashboards render no data; the pipeline behind them runs only in the US. Your logs are still in the EU. Read them with [Logpush](/logs/logpush/), and turn on [out-of-region access](/data-localization/log-region/out-of-region-access/) so your team can reach them from anywhere.

**Two prerequisites.** An EU log region requires [disabling Log Retention](/logs/logpull/enabling-log-retention/#turn-off-log-retention) on all zones first. Single-country processing regions have no failover; multi-country regions such as the EU keep the standard SLA.

Open defects are tracked on [Known issues](/data-localization/known-issues/).
