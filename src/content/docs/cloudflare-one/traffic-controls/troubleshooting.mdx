## Worker errors with the Forward action

If a Forward policy does not invoke your Worker or requests fail after enabling it, refer to [Forward troubleshooting](/cloudflare-one/traffic-controls/integrate-workers/forward-traffic/#troubleshooting). Worker invocation failures fail closed. Check HTTP filtering, TLS decryption, policy order, the named entrypoint, and Workers Logs before widening your policy scope.
