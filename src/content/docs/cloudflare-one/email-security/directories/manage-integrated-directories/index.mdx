To manage an integrated directory:

1. In the [Cloudflare dashboard](https://dash.cloudflare.com/), go to **Zero Trust** > **Email security** > **Directories**.

   <DashButton url="/?to=/:account/email-security/directories" zeroTrust />

2. Under **Directory name**, select your directory.
3. You will be redirected to a page where you can manage [Groups](/cloudflare-one/email-security/directories/manage-integrated-directories/manage-groups-directory/) or [Users](/cloudflare-one/email-security/directories/manage-integrated-directories/manage-users-directory/) directories.
