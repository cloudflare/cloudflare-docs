To view the destination addresses of user and team submissions:

1. In the [Cloudflare dashboard](https://dash.cloudflare.com/), go to **Zero Trust** > **Email security** > **Settings**.

   <DashButton url="/?to=/:account/email-security/settings" zeroTrust />

2. Go to **Phish submission** > **Submission addresses** > **View**.

The dashboard will display **User submission addresses** and **Team submission addresses**.
