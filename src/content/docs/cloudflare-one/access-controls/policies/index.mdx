Cloudflare Access Controls Policies allow you to secure your applications and resources by controlling who can access them and how they can be accessed.

- [Access policies](/cloudflare-one/access-controls/policies/access/) control who can reach your applications. You can create rules based on identity, device posture, and other factors to protect your internal applications.
- [API and Terraform](/cloudflare-one/access-controls/policies/api-terraform/) provide programmatic ways to manage your Access policies and configurations.
