Cloudflare Access Controls Policies allow you to secure your applications and resources by controlling who can access them and how they can be accessed.

- **Access policies** control who can reach your applications. You can create rules based on identity, device posture, and other factors to protect your internal applications.
- **API and Terraform** provide programmatic ways to manage your Access policies and configurations.
