## Optional Cloudflare settings

The application will default to the Cloudflare settings of the hostname in your account that includes the Cloudflare Tunnel DNS record, including [cache rules](/cache/how-to/cache-rules/) and [firewall policies](/firewall/). You can change these settings for your hostname in the Cloudflare dashboard.
