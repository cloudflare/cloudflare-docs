<Description>

Decouple event producers and consumers with a durable log

</Description>

<Plan type="workers-paid" />

:::note

K2 is in **public beta**, and any developer with a [Workers Paid plan](/workers/platform/pricing/) can start using it. During the beta, each account can store up to 10 GB. To request a higher limit, refer to [Limits](/k2/platform/limits/#account-limits).

:::

Cloudflare K2 is a durable log. Produce records to a log, store them for a configurable
retention period, and consume them from one or more independent consumers.

<LinkButton href="/k2/get-started/">Get started</LinkButton>

## Features

<Feature header="Create a K2 stream" href="/k2/get-started/">
	Create a K2 stream, produce records to it, and consume them through a
	subscription.
</Feature>

<Feature
	header="Produce with a Workers binding or HTTP"
	href="/k2/features/produce/"
	cta="Produce to K2"
>
	Produce records to a K2 stream with a Workers binding or the HTTP API.
</Feature>

<Feature
	header="Configure retention period"
	href="/k2/configuration/#retention"
>
	Records in K2 can be stored for up to 30 days, with longer retention available
	by request.
</Feature>

<Feature
	header="Create a subscription to consume"
	href="/k2/features/consume/"
	cta="Create a subscription"
>
	Share records across a group of consumers, or deliver every record to multiple
	independent consumers, with subscriptions.
</Feature>
