## Error 1010: The owner of this website has banned your access based on your browser's signature

This error indicates that access to the website is denied based on your browser's signature.

### Common causes

- [Browser Integrity Check](/waf/tools/browser-integrity-check/) is turned on by default for proxied zones and blocks requests whose `User-Agent` header matches known abusive or default non-browser signatures (for example, the default Python `urllib` user agent `Python-urllib/...`), even if the website owner did not configure a custom rule.
- A website owner explicitly blocked your request based on your client's `User-Agent` header.
- The request was sent to a `*.pages.dev` or `*.workers.dev` hostname, or to a custom domain of a Pages project, where Browser Integrity Check cannot be turned off. Refer to [Requests to Pages projects or workers.dev](#requests-to-pages-projects-or-workersdev).

### Resolution

- **If you control the HTTP client:** Configure your client to send a descriptive `User-Agent` header that identifies your application (for example, `my-app/1.0`) instead of an HTTP library default. The client does not need to impersonate a web browser.
- **If you own the website:** [Turn off Browser Integrity Check](/waf/tools/browser-integrity-check/#disable-browser-integrity-check) in Security **Settings**, or skip it selectively with a [WAF custom rule](/waf/custom-rules/skip/) or [configuration rule](/rules/configuration-rules/). This does not apply to `pages.dev` and `workers.dev` hostnames, or to custom domains of Pages projects. Refer to [Requests to Pages projects or workers.dev](#requests-to-pages-projects-or-workersdev).
- **If you are visiting someone else's website:** Notify the website owner of the blocking. If you cannot determine how to contact the website owner, look up contact information for the domain via the [Whois database](https://lookup.icann.org/).

:::note
Cloudflare support cannot override a customer's security settings. Browser Integrity Check on `pages.dev` and `workers.dev` cannot be turned off, either by the site owner or by Cloudflare support.
:::

### Requests to Pages projects or workers.dev

On a Cloudflare-managed zone, Browser Integrity Check also checks requests to `*.pages.dev` and `*.workers.dev` hostnames, and to custom domains of Pages projects. This happens even if you have turned off Browser Integrity Check, or skipped it with a custom rule or configuration rule, in your own zone. These blocks are not recorded in your zone's Security Events or in your Worker logs. In Security Events, you may only see your own skip rule match.

**Resolution:**

- Configure your client to send a descriptive `User-Agent` header that identifies your application (for example, `my-app/1.0`) instead of the library default. The client does not need to send a browser user agent.
- **Workers:** Serve the Worker on a [Custom Domain](/workers/configuration/routing/custom-domains/) or [route](/workers/configuration/routing/routes/) in your zone instead of `workers.dev`. Then turn off or skip Browser Integrity Check for the affected paths with a [configuration rule](/rules/configuration-rules/) or [custom rule](/waf/custom-rules/skip/). Refer to [workers.dev](/workers/configuration/routing/workers-dev/).
- **Pages:** Refer to [Pages known issues](/pages/platform/known-issues/).
