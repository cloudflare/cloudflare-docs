The previous modules cover how to set up your account securely. This module covers how to stay informed about what is happening in it. Audit logs give you a record of who did what and when. Notification policies alert you when something changes that you should know about. Together, they are your early warning system.

## Objectives

By the end of this module, you will be able to:

- Use audit logs to track account activity and investigate changes.
- Configure notification policies for security-relevant events.
