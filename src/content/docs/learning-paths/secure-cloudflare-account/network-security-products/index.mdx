Securing your account and developer resources covers the inside of your Cloudflare configuration. This module covers the products that protect what you expose to the internet: your domains, applications, and team members' access to internal tools.

The goal here is baseline configuration: the minimum you should have in place before considering your infrastructure secure. Each section links to the full product documentation for teams that want to go further.

## Objectives

By the end of this module, you will be able to:

- Configure Zero Trust Access to replace VPN for internal tool access.
- Apply DNSSEC and DNS best practices to all zones.
- Enable a WAF managed ruleset and DDoS protection baseline for public-facing zones.
