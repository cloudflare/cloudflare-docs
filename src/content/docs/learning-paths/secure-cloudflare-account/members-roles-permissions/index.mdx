Adding the right people with the right permissions is the most important ongoing security task for any Cloudflare account. Cloudflare's permission model is flexible, but that flexibility means the defaults are often broader than you want. This module covers how to read the model, pick the right roles for your team, scale with groups, and lock down access using SSO.

## Objectives

By the end of this module, you will be able to:

- Explain how Cloudflare's actor, scope, and role model works.
- Assign roles that follow the principle of least privilege for your team.
- Use user groups for scalable permission management.
- Configure SSO and SCIM for automated provisioning and deprovisioning.
