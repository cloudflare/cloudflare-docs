Data localization allows you to control where Cloudflare processes and stores your organization's data. This is important for meeting regulatory requirements and data sovereignty obligations.

## What is data localization?

Cloudflare's Data Localization Suite provides controls to:

- Choose which regions process your traffic
- Control where logs are stored
- Ensure data residency compliance
- Meet specific regulatory requirements (GDPR, data sovereignty laws, etc.)

## Configure data localization for Zero Trust

To configure data localization settings for your Zero Trust organization:

1. Review the available [data localization options](/data-localization/how-to/zero-trust/).
2. Determine which regions are appropriate for your compliance requirements.
3. Configure regional settings in your Zero Trust dashboard.

For detailed instructions, refer to [Data Localization for Zero Trust](/data-localization/how-to/zero-trust/).

## Considerations

- Data localization settings apply to all traffic processed through Cloudflare One
- Some features may have limitations based on regional availability
- Consult with your legal and compliance teams to determine appropriate settings
- Data localization may impact performance depending on user locations and selected regions
