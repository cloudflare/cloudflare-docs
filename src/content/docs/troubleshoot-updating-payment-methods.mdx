:::note
In order to manage your payment methods, you must have an [Account Role](/fundamentals/setup/manage-members/roles/) of Super Administrator or Billing.
::

# Unable to add or edit a payment method
You may not be able to add a new payment method or edit an existing payment method if your account already has too many payment methods added. The maximum number you can have is 2.

To solve this problem, you must remove some by following [Delete a payment method](/billing/update-billing-info/#delete-a-payment-method). Once you have done this, you can proceed to [Update payment method](/billing/update-billing-info/#update-payment-methods) or [Add a backup payment method](/billing/create-billing-profile/#add-a-backup-payment-method)
