**Category:** [Workers.dev Subdomains](/workers/platform/error-codes/#workers.dev-subdomains)

**HTTP Status:** 403 Forbidden

**Message:** `Email verification required. Verify your email address to use Workers.`

**When this occurs:**
Your Cloudflare account email is not verified.

**How to fix:**

1. Check your email for verification link
2. Resend verification from [account settings](https://dash.cloudflare.com/profile)

- [Verify Email](/fundamentals/setup/account/verify-email-address/)
