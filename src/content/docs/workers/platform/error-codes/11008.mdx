**Category:** [Bindings - Queues](/workers/platform/error-codes/#bindings--queues)

**HTTP Status:** 403 Forbidden

**Message:** `Queue limit reached: your account allows {limit} queues. Delete unused queues or upgrade your plan.`

**When this occurs:**
Your account has reached the maximum number of queues.

**How to fix:**
Delete unused queues.
