**Message:** Tracing is not enabled for your account. Enable Logpush to use this feature. See: https://developers.cloudflare.com/workers/observability/logs/logpush/


**Category:** [Observability](/workers/platform/error-codes/#observability)

**HTTP Status:** 403 Forbidden


**When this occurs:**
Your account lacks the logpush entitlement for tracing.
