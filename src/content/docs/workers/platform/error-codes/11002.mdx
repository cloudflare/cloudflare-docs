**Message:** Your account does not have access to Queues. Enable Queues at https://dash.cloudflare.com/?to=/:account/workers/queues


**Category:** [Bindings - Queues](/workers/platform/error-codes/#bindings--queues)

**HTTP Status:** 403 Forbidden


**When this occurs:**
Your account doesn't have Queues access.

**How to fix:**
[Enable Queues](https://dash.cloudflare.com/?to=/:account/workers/queues) for your account.
