**Category:** [Workers.dev Subdomains](/workers/platform/error-codes/#workers.dev-subdomains)

**HTTP Status:** 403 Forbidden

**Message:** `Subdomain '{subdomain}' is unavailable. Choose a different subdomain name and try again.`

**When this occurs:**
The requested workers.dev subdomain is already taken or reserved.

**How to fix:**
Choose a different subdomain name.
