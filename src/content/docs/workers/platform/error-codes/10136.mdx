**Category:** [Bindings - R2](/workers/platform/error-codes/#bindings--r2)

**HTTP Status:** 400 Bad Request

**Message:** `R2 binding error for bucket '{bucket}': {details}. Review the error and try again.`

**When this occurs:**
There's a configuration error with your R2 binding.

**How to fix:**
Review the error details and verify your R2 binding configuration.
