**Category:** [Observability](/workers/platform/error-codes/#observability)

**HTTP Status:** 403 Forbidden

**Message:** `Tail Workers require a Workers Paid plan. Upgrade at https://dash.cloudflare.com/?to=/:account/workers/plans`

**How to fix:**

[Upgrade to Workers Paid](https://dash.cloudflare.com/?to=/:account/workers/plans) to use Tail Workers.

**Alternatives for free plan:**

- Use `wrangler tail` for real-time debugging during development
- Export logs via [OpenTelemetry](/workers/observability/otel/) to your observability platform
- Use `console.log()` and view in the Workers dashboard

See [Tail Workers](/workers/observability/logs/tail-workers/) for setup instructions.
