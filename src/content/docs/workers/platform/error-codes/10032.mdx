**Category:** [Workers.dev Subdomains](/workers/platform/error-codes/#workers.dev-subdomains)

**HTTP Status:** 404 Not Found

**Message:** `Subdomain '{subdomain}' is available but not configured. Visit the Workers dashboard to claim it, then try again.`

**When this occurs:**
Querying a subdomain that exists but isn't set up for your account.

**How to fix:**
Open the [Workers dashboard](https://dash.cloudflare.com/?to=/:account/workers) to automatically create your subdomain.
