**Message:** Your account does not have access to R2. Enable R2 at https://dash.cloudflare.com/?to=/:account/r2


**Category:** [Bindings - R2](/workers/platform/error-codes/#bindings--r2)

**HTTP Status:** 403 Forbidden


**When this occurs:**
Your account doesn't have R2 access.

**How to fix:**
[Enable R2](https://dash.cloudflare.com/?to=/:account/r2) for your account.
