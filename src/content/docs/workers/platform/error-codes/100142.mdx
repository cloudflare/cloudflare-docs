**Message:** mTLS certificate (id) is missing the private key. Upload a certificate that includes the private key and try again.


**Category:** [Bindings - Other](/workers/platform/error-codes/#bindings--other)

**HTTP Status:** 400 Bad Request


**How to fix:**
Upload a certificate that includes the private key.
