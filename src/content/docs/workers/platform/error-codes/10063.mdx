**Message:** A workers.dev subdomain is required. Visit the Workers dashboard to create one automatically, then try again.


**Category:** [Workers.dev Subdomains](/workers/platform/error-codes/#workers.dev-subdomains)

**HTTP Status:** 403 Forbidden


**When this occurs:**
The operation requires a workers.dev subdomain but your account doesn't have one.

**How to fix:**
Open the [Workers dashboard](https://dash.cloudflare.com/?to=/:account/workers) to automatically create your subdomain.
