**Category:** [Bindings - Queues](/workers/platform/error-codes/#bindings--queues)

**HTTP Status:** 403 Forbidden

**Message:** `Queues are not available on the free plan. Upgrade to Workers Paid at https://dash.cloudflare.com/?to=/:account/workers/plans`

**When this occurs:**
Queues require a paid Workers plan.

**How to fix:**
[Upgrade to Workers Paid](https://dash.cloudflare.com/?to=/:account/workers/plans).
