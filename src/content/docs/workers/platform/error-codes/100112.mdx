**Category:** [Custom Domains](/workers/platform/error-codes/#custom-domains)

**HTTP Status:** 400 Bad Request

**Message:** `Hostname '{hostname}' does not belong to zone '{zone}'. Use a hostname from this zone and try again.`

**When this occurs:**
The custom domain hostname isn't part of the specified zone.

**How to fix:**
Use a hostname that's part of the zone (same domain or subdomain).
