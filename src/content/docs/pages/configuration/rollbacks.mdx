Rollbacks allow you to instantly revert your project to a previous production deployment. For rollbacks and gradual deployments on Cloudflare Workers, refer to [Workers Rollbacks](/workers/versions-and-deployments/rollbacks/).

Any production deployment that has been successfully built, and has not been deleted, is a valid rollback target. Deployments older than 30 days are deleted, except your 100 most recent and your current production deployment, and you cannot roll back to a deleted deployment. For more information, refer to [Deployments limits](/pages/platform/limits/#deployments). When your project has rolled back to a previous deployment, you may still roll back to deployments that are newer than your current version. Note that preview deployments are not valid rollback targets.

To perform a rollback:

<Steps>
1. In the Cloudflare dashboard, go to the **Workers & Pages** page.

   <DashButton url="/?to=/:account/workers-and-pages" />
2. Select your Pages project and go to **Deployments**.
3. Browse the **All deployments** list, select the three-dot actions menu for the desired target, and select **Rollback to this deployment**.
4. Confirm in the dialog window to update your production deployment.
</Steps>
