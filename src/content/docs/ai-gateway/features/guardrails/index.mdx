AI Gateway Guardrails is a content safety and [prompt injection](https://www.cloudflare.com/learning/ai/prompt-injection/) filter that sits between your application and your [model providers](/ai-gateway/usage/providers/). It evaluates user prompts and model responses in real time using [`@cf/meta/llama-guard-3-8b`](/workers-ai/models/llama-guard-3-8b/) for content hazard categories (`S1` to `S14`) and `@cf/meta/prompt-guard-2-86m` for prompt injection (`P1`), then flags, blocks, or ignores each category based on your gateway settings.

Because Guardrails runs at the proxy layer, you define one safety policy and enforce it consistently across OpenAI, Anthropic, DeepSeek, Workers AI, and other supported providers. Blocked requests return structured error codes (`2016` for prompts, `2017` for responses), and every evaluation is recorded in AI Gateway Logs for review and auditing.

With AI Gateway Guardrails, you get:

- **Cross-provider enforcement**: Apply one set of content safety and prompt-injection rules across all models routed through AI Gateway.
- **Per-category actions**: Configure each hazard category (`S1` to `S14`) and prompt injection (`P1`) independently to **Flag**, **Block**, or **Ignore** for prompts and responses.
- **Real-time blocking and error codes**: Stop disallowed prompts before they reach a model provider and block unsafe responses before they reach your users, with distinct error codes your application can handle.
- **Audit logs**: Review logged prompts, model responses, and Guardrail verdicts in AI Gateway Logs to support internal oversight and compliance checks.

## How Guardrails work

AI Gateway inspects requests and responses in real time against your configured safety categories. Guardrails operate in three steps:

1. **Intercept traffic**: AI Gateway proxies requests between your application and the upstream AI provider.
2. **Evaluate prompts and responses**:
   - **User prompts**: Checked for content hazards (`S1` to `S14`) and prompt injection (`P1`). Prompts that match a blocked category are stopped before reaching the model.
   - **Model responses**: Checked for content hazards (`S1` to `S14`) after the provider responds. Responses that match a blocked category are stopped before being returned to the caller.
3. **Apply configured actions**: Flagged interactions proceed and are logged for review. Blocked interactions are rejected with error code `2016` (prompt) or `2017` (response).

For setup steps, category codes, and streaming behavior, refer to [Set up Guardrails](/ai-gateway/features/guardrails/set-up-guardrail/), [Supported model types](/ai-gateway/features/guardrails/supported-model-types/), and [Usage considerations](/ai-gateway/features/guardrails/usage-considerations/).
